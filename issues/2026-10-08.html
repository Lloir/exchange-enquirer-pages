Pulse of the Galaxy

Auction-drunk, with a hangover booked for tomorrow

The executive auction house has the galaxy in a fever. Since the paper last reported on the five per cent cap, sellers have kept finding out about it the hard way, buyers are bragging about nine-figure bids and owning up to the mistakes in the same sentence, and Medici admitted he was trying not to look at the auction house in case he went trigger happy on execs he doesn't need. Zinc's line about realising you're 1.5b in the red and the execs aren't much better sums up the mood nicely: lots of fun, lots of regret, all at once.

Underneath the spending, chat is in a playful, slightly unhinged mood. One player took another's name, there was a bogus murder inquiry into GP, a 184-strong chicken listing, a multilingual spam wave, and the usual scuffle over whose guild arcade is bigger. Nobody seemed especially worried about anything apart from storage space and overproduced flux. The natives are restless, but mostly because they're bored and loaded.

BOTH OF THEM

WHO'S THE REAL M'KAURIA? NAME NICKED IN BROAD DAYLIGHT

Makuria Inc toyed with a silly new name for under a minute, and someone else got there first.

By the Enquirer's identity theft desk

It began, as these things do, with a typo. SL Refining misspelt Makuria as 'mkauria', Farmy declared it a 'new name', Unicorn Industries upgraded it to 'm'kauria', and Makuria Inc, who should have known better, admitted being 'tempted to change to M'kauria' before snapping 'no fuuck off its my name'. A firm stance. It held for roughly zero minutes.

NOT ANYMORE ITS NOT

M'kauria

By 13:42 an account called M'kauria was in chat announcing 'NOT ANYMORE ITS NOT', and the log helpfully shows SL Refining's earlier lines now appearing under the new name. Chat drew its own conclusions, and so has this newspaper. Makuria answered by renaming to M'kauria Flux Consortium, reporting 'name successively changed'. We think he meant successfully, but successively fits too, as the day went on.

What came next was an identity crisis on a galactic scale. Bob Corp groaned 'Oh great, now there's two of them', Farmy complained that the matching icons were 'messing with my head', and the original admitted that seeing his 'bootleg name and logo in chat is trippy'. The copy insisted 'im the real one' and offered 'go to our dms youll see' as proof, which convinced nobody in any court we know of.

Sparky had a go at shutting it down with 'devs ban for multi account kthxbai', and the Enquirer would like to point out, before anyone rushes off with screenshots, that these are two separate people sharing one bad idea. By mid-afternoon Pear Inc was announcing 'M'kauria is in tit now', only for the guild to turn out to be full. So the fake M'kauria finished the day with a stolen name and no new guild, and the real one finished it as a flux consortium. We'd call that a draw. The rename is still in place at the time of printing.

CAPPED

SEVEN HUNDRED MILLION? NOT ON THIS CV, SUNSHINE

RAGE Inc woke up thinking he was a billionaire. Chat delivered the bad news before breakfast.

By the Enquirer's told-you-so desk

RAGE Inc. turned up at 13:18 to announce he was 'becoming billionaire in couple of hours', with an executive called Hendrik Bauer bidding at 700M. Farmy's reply was instant and unkind: 'you won't get 700M'. Readers of our previous edition already know how this ends.

Makuria explained that the payout is capped at 5% of CV, 'so you can't just boost', and that the rest 'goes to the fuucking feds'. Farmy thought it was 10% and called the whole thing 'yet another money sink'. RAGE took it like a pro: 'damn xd'.

He wasn't the only one. That morning SpaceCo. wanted to know why an exec that sold for 75mil paid out just 19mil. Iconian Agricultural answered in two words, 'your CV', and Voidsteel set out the 80 per cent cut and the cap. Two days running now. The Enquirer is thinking about printing the rule on the masthead.

BERLIN'S BARGAIN BIN BONANZA

One small distributor says he's made a fortune buying the execs nobody else wanted.

By our auction house correspondent

While the big spenders fretted, Berlin food distributions quietly claimed to be on '39/26 employed', which he says comes from bidding on more execs than he has slots for while keeping one spot open. Dumb wanted to know how that was even possible. So did we.

His running total grew all evening: 100mil profit plus 100+ execs at 19:26, a promise of 200mil 'in about 20 minutes', 200mil from the auction house at 19:51, then at 20:08 a revised '165mil cash + 175 execs'. Either the books are being carefully reconciled or the numbers are going the wrong way. The Enquirer simply notes the figures as given.

His secret, he says: 'I mostly buy cheap execs about to expire', plus being 'chronically online'. Voidsteel called it 'actually quite good', especially for his size, then called it 'Trash' a couple of minutes later. Our verdict: decent, and slightly suspicious maths.

BUYER'S REMORSE CLUB HOLDS FIRST MEETING

Big spenders own up to nine-figure punts on execs they didn't need.

By the Enquirer's confessional

Voidsteel Conglomerate admitted paying 220m at most, 'kind of a mistake', plus 'like 50m on an exec that in the end is not usefull', and estimated 500m spent overall before correcting himself to 'way more'. M'kauria Flux Consortium put up 111m and is relying on the devs adding drugs. Medici expects to stay under 1b 'if the next 2 goes through', one of them a thank-you bid for a supplier, which is lovely and also expensive.

then u realise ur 1.5b in the red and the execs arent much better

Zinc's refinery

Zinc's refinery bought a couple of worker burden execs for 200m and then realised that 'having 50 bases is absolutely horrendous for exec roi'. That's an expensive way to learn it, but at least it's honest.

For anyone shopping, Ares Inc. offered the most useful advice of the day: minus base burden and minus admin burden are 'the two universally best traits', and '+prod isnt actually that good'. Ares says swapping 6 or 7 execs cut about 100% off modified overhead. Frugal Factories says he saw one exec go for $1 billion, and nobody could remember why. Meanwhile Wakawulawuha's Sirius Grant is heading past 80mil and Layla Saleh is 'never' for sale, which is exactly what people say before they sell.

Exchange weather

Heavy executive showers right across the auction house, with sudden cold fronts wherever the five per cent cap meets a 700M bid. Expect a flux flood tomorrow, as Makuria forecasts overproduction, while Quantum Research Data is picking up again according to Pass 4 Denied.

CASE DISMISSED

GP FINDS GP INNOCENT OF ALL CHARGES

Voidsteel demands a murder inquiry and is invited to join the suspects.

By the Enquirer's crime and courts desk

At 19:47 Voidsteel Conglomerate formally requested an investigation into 'various killings' inside GP headquarters, claiming members had been 'lured in with false promises of high level guild buildings'. It was very convincing for a man who had just been sent a pretend hit by Zinc ('voidsteel is your target. no witnesses.').

GP's justice system worked at speed. M'kauria: 'request denied'. Zinc's refinery: 'We have investigated ourselves and found no wrongdoing.' Medici said a formal investigation needed a formal application to join GP, and Zinc proposed three weeks in federal prison for libel if Voidsteel didn't sign up. Skippy refused the contract on the grounds that targeting his 'own kangaroo brother is prohibited'. That's the most legally sound thing anyone said all day.

Before all that, Voidsteel had floated the theory that TiT is a front set up in secret to boost Twilight, citing Twilight's 200B CV and his doubts that it came from selling Grain. To be clear, this was a joke, and Medici's 'secret boosting technique' quip was too. The Enquirer reports the theory only as a theory, and as a fairly good bit of comedy.

LEK ADMITS GHOSTS RIGGED IN GP'S FAVOUR

The gt-c creator confesses his most unfair act, then ships an update anyway.

By our tools and gadgets reporter

Chat spent the evening calling Lek 'the GT messiah' and joking that he's secretly the game's creator, which he took with good grace before making a shocking confession. In gt-c's GP vs ATS minigame, the ghosts are quicker when you play as GP. '#rigged', he added, as if he were proud of it. Cracklor called it 'awesome'. Nobody has opened an investigation, which after GP's evening seems wise.

When you're playing as GP, the ghost move like 20% faster or so

Lek

Earlier he helped CCCP, whose plans kept adding about 55m of apex materials to the wishlist. Sumran Consortium got there first by asking whether the HQ upgrade was included, and it was. Rearden Steel also pointed Amrin Baddie.Inc to the workforce tools to check whether Stanley Holloway was worth having. He wasn't, so back on the auction he went.

The update went live at 21:11, and an hour later Farmy asked for the site to be easier to navigate. Lek pointed him to CTRL+K and the prices button in the navbar. Farmy said the site doesn't tell you that. Lek said both are in the navbar. Cracklor reckoned gt-c is 'still updating faster then the debs'. Overall the Enquirer finds a fan-made tool getting better reviews than the game it serves, which says plenty about both.

DEATH OF ATS 'NEEDS TO BE STUDIED'

A guild's members stage its funeral in public chat, still wearing the tags.

By the Enquirer's obituaries desk

Bob Corp floated abducting two sparring players into ATS for a week, and Cracklor answered that 'ATS IS THE DEVIL' for kicking 'our boy Coss'. Ares Inc. asked 'ATS who?', Voidsteel wondered whether it still existed, and Skippy said its downfall 'needs to be studied'. Bob Corp took responsibility: 'Alas, it was all my fault :('. Our condolences, sort of.

Lek, still wearing the tag, announced 'ATS died. Time to pack up', while Sumran Consortium, another ATS member, was told to 'go back into timeout' after suggesting a GTC guild where everyone has to donate weekly for gt-c access. Medici asked whether there would be another funeral and wanted one for GP too. For a dead guild, ATS has a remarkably loud wake.
