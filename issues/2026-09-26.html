BREAKING BAD: Coss cooks up chem comeback, Tato mans the barricades

PvP's one-man army says he is respeccing into chemistry for a bit, and ATS's Tato Industries has turned stopping him into a hobby.

By the Enquirer's hazardous materials desk

It began at 16:12 with a boast and ended at midnight with a duel. Coss, flying the PvP flag, told chat that hundreds of billions in liquid capital can do a lot, and that he could make money without buildings by controlling markets. Holue's Galatic Contruction was not convinced, saying that in his opinion market manipulation cannot really be done because the big players have guild suppliers, and that the devs stepped in last time only because small players would have been hurt.

i don't have any hard feelings, but i have made a game out of making sure you don't make any money off chem

Tato Industries

Coss claimed the devs took action last time because they knew he was going to destroy the game for a week. The Enquirer cannot check that and prints it as his claim. Then at 16:37 he posted a notice: Chemistry research completed x60, 'Just to start'. SL Refining, who a few minutes earlier had been told he was indestructible, took it personally: 'I should've known his PvP would start with me.'

By late evening the plan was clearer. Coss said a couple of folks had convinced him to stay and try something new, and that he was respeccing into chem, only to level 60, for three weeks or so while he builds up his walls. 'Nothing personal,' he added. The numbers were simply the highest.

Tato Industries, who clearly lives in the chem neighbourhood, gave a very calm declaration of war. Tato said Coss's meddling had forced him to keep stockpiles 'which i use to defend my realm', admitted to probably having more fun than usual, and said he would rather make things hard for Coss even if it meant suffering himself. Coss answered 'hehe challenge accepted <3'. Nobody has won yet. Watch this space, and your chem listings.

Cracklor Logistics summed it up at 23:59: in a game of profits, some people are not seeking profits.

NOT ADOPTED

Mob justice! Chat votes to jail union man Holue

A proposal to boycott stars until the auction house arrives ends with a show of hands for federal prison.

By our court reporter, sitting in the public gallery

At 16:48 Holue's Galatic Contruction had what he called a genius idea: boycott buying stars until the devs ship the auction house. 'We must unionize,' he told chat, insisting it would be out by next week if everyone stuck together.

Voting people into prison sounds like a system already out of whack. Glad it isn't adopted.

Frugal Factories

The response was swift and not very union-friendly. SL Refining said to give the devs money so they can afford development. Coss called the idea a bannable offence and suggested a week in federal prison. Echelon Interstellar Group proposed that everyone then throw pie at him. SL Refining raised the bid to one day of prison per 'clueless chat'.

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th turned up later to ask who was being voted in and tallied '+1 for holololol, +1 for SL'. SL Refining objected that a cell shared with Holue would be double prison, then asked whether he would at least get copies of the paper inside. Murderous Trading Corps offered organ-related advice that this paper will not be repeating.

The voice of reason was Frugal Factories, who pointed out that a random mob sending someone to prison without law or evidence is a system out of whack, and patiently explained to Zinc's refinery that 'federal prison' meant the in-game one. Nobody was actually jailed.

Meanwhile Sparky of RNK pitched a different route to the auction house: a rumour that joining RNK, donating your 7% and leaving would convince the devs to hurry up. The Enquirer reports this as a recruitment attempt, not as dev policy.

Page 4Business

Admin centre apartheid? Chat does the maths on guild perks

Top producers say GP and ATS admin centres give them a head start that nobody else can catch.

By the Enquirer's league tables correspondent

A late-night chat about how Ignis made top 10 in construction turned into a debate on admin centres. Echelon Interstellar Group said only GP and ATS have admin centres good enough to support a top 10 company, and put numbers on it: moving from ATS's admin centre to TiT's would cost them $105M a day in profit, and moving to GR's would cost $280M.

Hey, I'm top 10 and TiT admin center is plenty for me

Ares Inc.

Cracklor Logistics said even his small setup sees about a 150m a day gap between TiT and other guilds. Pear Inc. argued that TiT had poured its own money in from active members, without the boost of legacy members quitting and dumping their whole CV into the pot like the two bigger guilds, and said this needs regulating.

Medici Mining Conglomerate, for GP, said anyone can technically do it given time, which Holue greeted with 'of course a gp person would say that'. Coss reckoned that with an extra 80 levels or so, any big GP player outside the top 10 is just slacking.

Not everyone was complaining. Ares Inc. said the TiT admin centre was plenty for a top 10 player, then admitted another 100 levels would come in handy.

Water baron eyes $100B party

Echelon's secret side hustle turns out to be very cheap water.

By our utilities editor

Echelon Interstellar Group announced 3.1M workers first thing ('Rawr') and was $2.2B short of a $100B milestone by 22:43. Coss suggested crashing the water market to spoil the party and worked out that halving the water average would set Echelon back 3B+.

Delay my $100B party by a whole day or so

Echelon Interstellar Group

Echelon revealed that water is a second income stream, bought at $13 from other players over the past month, and invited everyone to list their water at $13. The worst case, Echelon worked out, was delaying the $100B party by a whole day or so. Having 'too much cash' and wanting 'more entertainment', Echelon is now looking for a new market to abuse. Suggestions from Cracklor Logistics included buying up the whole antimatter chain, which Echelon said Twilight and Tato already have locked up.

Nanoshield hard sell as Sumran stocks up

SL Refining wakes up rich and starts shouting.

By the Enquirer's defence procurement desk

SL Refining opened the afternoon asking why he had woken up to 3b in cash, and spent a good part of the evening shouting 'EVEYONE BUY MY NANOSHIELDS'. The pitch landed at least once: Sumran Consortium mentioned buying about 150k shielding in the last 24 hours, to which SL replied that you could always use more.

Cracklor Logistics also found himself with 3b and ran a poll on what to do with it. Echelon and Coss both voted for more chickens. Pear Inc. suggested a guild crawl and donating the lot.

Exchange weather

Chickens are out of the green for the first time in a long while, according to Skye Blu Foods, and ale is running hot, according to Misriah armory. Chem, which Tato reports has nosedived, faces a front of incoming Coss with scattered stockpiles. Water holds steady at $13, but only because one buyer keeps it there.

NO COMMENT

Paper gets reviewed, and the readers are brutal

Chat holds a public review of this very publication. We are printing it anyway, out of bravery.

By the Enquirer's navel-gazing correspondent

At 14:54 Holue's Galatic Contruction called the paper a general chat regurgitator and said, in what he admitted was his very biased opinion, that only the markets page was worth reading. Farmy McFarm Face The 7th defended the build, saying the content is AI-generated but the website is all hand-written CSS and HTML plus the scripts behind it, and that the coffee donations only cover server upkeep.

If you cancel it they win, can't have that

EggInASac

The readers' panel was split. EggInASac said the newspaper is fun. Lek said they preferred what Holue has been writing so far. Misriah armory said it was still a good paper even if AI made it, and added 'call me a clanker but i like farmys'. Zinc's refinery thought it had a lot of TiT-speak. Pear Inc. then summed things up in a sentence the Enquirer's lawyers asked us to paraphrase: everyone is annoying and should have a beer.

Earlier Cracklor Logistics suggested going weekly with special editions on busy days, noted that weekly would mean a crossword every time, and shared the secret of getting into the paper: you don't ask, you just fill chat with your ramblings. When Farmy mused about cancelling the paper altogether, Murderous Trading Corps and EggInASac both told him not to.

Guild crawler Pear goes F4T

The TiT regular turns into a travelling fruit.

By our society pages, wearing a bib

Pear Inc. announced at 12:44 that he was 'hoboing again'. Offers came from Murderous Trading Corps (go to GTF), Farmy (get Holue's GR to recruit you) and S.T.A.R Labs ('Cmere pear'). By 13:11 the verdict was in, shouted in capitals.

I'M A F4T PEAR

Pear Inc.

Void LLC thought an earlier spill might be connected to the move. Pear insisted he would never leave a mess. Frugal Factories asked whether F4T members now eat pear while they contemplate, and Farmy confirmed that they do.

By night Pear was talking about crawling to GP next and applying to PvP in the morning, but would not rank the guilds he has visited, saying he would not insult his hosts.

Small wins: free food, second bases and a first big ship

While the big players argued about billions, the little guys got things done.

By the Enquirer's good news desk

Sheoix Heavy Industries woke to find their workers had run out of food overnight. super cool company offered to send some over, and Sheoix said it was more than plenty. Cracklor Logistics called it 'so damn wholesome', and this paper agrees.

Noid Inc. got base 2 running. Murderous Trading Corps teased them about doing it at 10 mil CV, but Coss said doubling your planets is no small feat. Noid admitted they had overbuilt their first base. Q Enterprises reported technically reaching a first 1mt ship, and Make Stuff INC is close to its first level 10s.

Make Stuff INC also asked what O&O was three times over the day. The answer finally came at 19:51: Murderous Trading Corps said it was Flip's game, and Holue posted the address. GreaVroes LLC had already reported getting softlocked in its farming tutorial after putting an extractor on sand instead of water.
