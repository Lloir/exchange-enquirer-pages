Pulse of the Galaxy

Popcorn out, eyes on the referee

Saturday's galaxy was a crowd that had stopped watching the match and started watching the officials. The boosting row didn't get people angry at Space Inc so much as at the size of the fine: Frugal Factories did sums about getting caught now and then, Pear Inc. said he knew several players banned for receiving boosts, and Ares Inc. shouted for someone to boost him 7B so he could 'EXPOSE THE HYPROCRACY'. Skippy posted the popcorn emoji at least four times, which is about the right ratio.

Under the noise there's a low hum of boredom and guild-hopping. Farmy told a dev outright that he reset 'BECAUSE THE GAME IS BORING', Sumran said ATS is shrinking to 15, Ares said he's done, and half of TiT spent the evening shopping for day passes to other guilds. The newcomers are still turning up, though (Pendamen asking about rim logistics, Errantes Inc. asking for a 'take all' button, copany pez glova saying hello), so the shop isn't shut. The regulars just want something new to do.

FINED, ALLEGEDLY

SEVEN BILLION REASONS TO BE CROSS: COSS CALLS TIME ON SPACE INC

A growth chart shoots up 20-odd times, a report gets filed, and a pasted DM says the fine came to 8, which chat promptly decided was cheap.

By the Enquirer's courts and spreadsheets desk

At 15:46 Coss asked general chat whether GP was 'openly boosting space inc to 100B before he quits', pointing to what he called 20x normal growth in a day. Two minutes later Pass 4 Denied said he had already reported it through the in-game function and tagged the devs. This paper notes that the accused, GP's Space Inc., said nothing in chat all day, so everything here comes from the people pointing at him.

If the penalty for $7B boosting is $8B ... people only need to get away with cheating once in awhile to make it profitable.

Frugal Factories

Things soon went sideways, as they tend to when Coss is involved. Skippy said 'It’s only Coss that complains about others daily', Coss replied that he had subsidised more into ATS in a week than Skippy's entire CV, and SL Refining offered the theory that if lots of people have reported someone and nothing has happened, he's 'probably legit'. A brave position to take with half the facts, which SL admitted ('idk anything about this').

Then at 16:26 Coss pasted what he said was his exchange with dev Builder Inc.: 'He was warned and fined. Thanks for the report.' Asked whether the fine covered the 7B CV, the reply he pasted was simply '8'. The Enquirer can't see the devs' inbox, so this is Coss's paste, not a system notice. Later that night Coss said Space Inc 'got actioned' and his science was on the market, and Echelon Interstellar Group called it 'Cheap science day today'. Make of that what you will. Plenty did.

The verdict from the stands was less about the crime than the price. Frugal Factories argued an 8B fine on a 7B boost only works out cheap if you get away with it most of the time, Farmy added 'shame boosting fines aren't hefty', and at 20:11 Cracklor summed up the mood: boost new players, ban; boost big players, meh. GreaVroes LLC did the sums for the little guy (an 8B fine would leave a new player on -7.9B CV), and Ares Inc. asked to be boosted 7B so he could get banned and prove the point. Nobody did him the favour.

Newcomer Errantes Inc. asked, in his words, whether one-off massive boosts are bad but 'milking dozens of people for whole year' is fine. Farmy said 'yes'. The Enquirer leaves that one with the reader.

The Enquirer Says

THE ENQUIRER SAYS: A FINE ISN'T A PUNISHMENT IF IT'S A FEE

We're not a court and we don't play one in chat, so we're taking Coss's pasted DM as exactly that: a paste. But if the fine really was 8B on a 7B gain, then Frugal Factories is right and the littering comparison holds. If a fine costs a few percent more than the crime paid, it's a toll, not a deterrent, and every big account in the galaxy can do that maths.

Pear Inc. made the point that should worry the devs most: he said he knew several players banned for receiving boosts. Whether that's fair or not, if even part of chat thinks small players get the axe and big ones get an invoice, you can't fix that with a quiet DM reply. Our unsolicited advice: whatever the policy is, say it in public, apply it the same way to everyone, and let chat find something else to argue about. Ideally crayons.

— The Editor

NINTH TIME LUCKY: CLONE FARMY STILL CAN'T BUY A PINT

Two previous Farmys lost to a saw blade and a vat, a court date pending, and a dev with jokes.

By our coroner's correspondent

When YoYo Ltd. asked why it was suddenly Farmy Mc Farm Face the 9th, the explanation was grim: the 7th 'got in an accident with Drone', and the 8th 'didn't make it out of the cloning vat'. Drone inc. didn't exactly deny it, saying PvP guild infrastructure 'assumes basic competence' and Farmy should have seen the saw blade coming, then added that he had to practise for the court hearing. Farmy, handily, has no memories of any of it. Very convenient for everyone.

BECAUSE THE GAME IS BORING

Farmy Mc Farm Face the 9th

The new clone's main grievance is the free trade limit. He asked someone to send him 26 Ale, confirmed he's 'Still getting carded', and later complained 'I STILL can't buy ale'. A fresh start on the cloning slab and still no pint. Cruel.

Then came the moment of the day. Farmy asked dev Builder Inc. to bump his CV to 200 million, and Builder Inc. asked why he reset if he wanted to be rich, with a grin. Drone called it a 'Rare dev-burn', and this paper agrees. Farmy did at least report he was 60k off his half-mil mark by the evening, so the 9th is getting somewhere.

FLUX CAPACITOR: MAKURIA'S EMPIRE HAS A RIVAL AT THE DOOR

Four in ten flux units, a million limestone a day at K2, and Cracklor wants a piece.

By the Enquirer's heavy industry desk

Asked by Medici how much of the flux market he makes, Makuria Inc gave the figure 10.6m out of 25.6m, which by our maths is over 40 per cent, and he still grumbled that others had snuck in more production while he was 'distracted with sulfuric acid and fert'. Fair enough. Most of the galaxy can't keep track of one product.

everytime I start breaking free void just smelts more

Makuria Inc

The empire runs on rock: K2 alone uses 'just over a million limestone', and he gave his daily limestone use as 4.7m. Meanwhile Cracklor Logistics. announced he's making 500k flux a day, called himself Coss's competition in metal and promised to 'dominate him'. Ares Inc. was thrilled to learn he's the biggest flux consumer ('wooooooooo #1').

Makuria then agreed to send flux to Coss on contract at $100. Cracklor, ever helpful, warned that 'last time coss booshed someone they got banned'. Makuria reckoned they were both 'more than big enough it wouldn't matter', which given the front page is a sentence we'll be keeping on file. As for leaving SRS for TiT: Makuria says Void keeps him chained up, and every time he gets loose Void just smelts more.

Exchange weather

Coffee outlook heavy, with YoYo Ltd. reporting 30M of volume on the market. A front of cheap science may roll in off GP's waters following events on our front page. Flux stays settled under a high pressure system centred on Makuria, with Cracklor coming up fast from the south.

ATS ENTRANCE EXAM: SPREADSHEETS, CONCRETE, AND NO TALKING

Guild reported going down to 15 as Echelon, Sumran and Tato show applicants the door.

By our admissions office correspondent

Asked late in the evening whether ATS is dying, officer Echelon Interstellar Group answered with a recruitment policy: 'There’s a spreadsheet exam to join ATS.' SL Refining asked to sit it and was told he'd get 'a different exam'. The one practical question Echelon gave out was to buy 35M concrete and sell it to him for $30. We don't know of anyone passing it, and that's probably the point.

my rule of thumb is, i won't accept you if you have ever typed 5 consecutive messages in this general chat

Tato Industries

Sumran Consortium was more direct: 'we are going down to 15 people.' He said nobody is being kicked, they just aren't recruiting, and he'd like guild buildings deleted and replaced with galaxy-wide ones, which Echelon said should come with disco balls. Earlier Drone inc. had described ATS guild chat as 'pretty dead', and Sumran had replied that they'd picked up 'a few guild chat yappers'. Skippy said he'd applied and heard nothing back.

Then Tato Industries arrived with the toughest admissions rule this paper has seen, and told SL Refining on the spot that he'd failed. Rip all of TiT, said Sumran. We'd point out that this paper exists because people type five messages in a row, so we're a little hurt too.

PASS THE CROWN: SL REFINING'S BRIEF AND BENEVOLENT REIGN

TiT's leadership changes hands more often than its contracts, and nobody has had to stage a coup.

By the Enquirer's royal correspondent

At 14:53 SL Refining addressed his 'LOYAL SUBJECTS', promised to wipe their memories out of benevolence, and made his first decree that everyone must show their love for Lek. Murderous Trading Corps answered with a one-word insult, and SL announced MTC 'gets banned'. Holue asked whether SL had won an election or this was 'one of those TiT leadership swaps'. Good question, Holue.

Im running to ats

SL Refining

By the evening the reign had fallen apart. SL said 'Farmy asked for it back' and 'It went downhill from there', Cracklor reminded SL he'd left 'of your own accord', and when Cracklor asked Farmy for the GM role back, Farmy said 'no' and declared himself Caesar. Aldera Mining Company called TiT 'the easiest guilt to coup', GreaVroes LLC asked whether it counts as a coup if they hand you the leadership, and Cracklor insisted no coup has ever happened in TiT. Going by today, nobody's ever needed one.

At 22:40 AMY posted an appeal: 'our SL Refining escaped again. please help us catch him.' SL said he was heading to ATS. See above for how that went.

THE EDITOR WILL SEE YOU NOW (THE EDITOR IS THE PAPER)

Mittcb writes in asking for a name change; chat weighs in on his handwriting.

Elsewhere on the subject of us: S.T.A.R Labs announced 'I got my own section in the newspaperr', Void LLC found out there's a paper and then declared it 'lowkey kinda peak', and Farmy linked a company apparently called The Editor, which Pass 4 Denied pointed out doesn't mention it in its bio. We're touched. We're also not that company.
