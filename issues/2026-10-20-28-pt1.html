The Enquirer's Tips For New Players: PIFFs Company's Dilemma

PIFFs Company, a newcomer to the game, receives unexpected advice on company growth.

By the Exchange Desk

New player PIFFs Company sought advice on his startup strategy, but received mixed reactions. Some players suggested sticking with missions early on, while others offered a bank idea involving trade imbalances.

im making concrete

PiFFs Company

However, Cucklor Logistics and his friends threw cold water on concrete manufacturing efforts, leading to a humorous debate on the game's tutorial. PIFFs Company was then advised to scrap their mining efforts and import materials instead.

The Enquirer's advice? Stick with the basics, master those, and then branch out. Starting too big too early can be costly.

Guild Drama At Its Best

Cucklor Logistics and friends debate guild values and ethics over a chicken and bunny feast.

By the Enquirer's desk

Guild drama is alive and well in the General chat. Players like Cucklor Logistics and others were caught in a heated debate over bunnies and chickens. Cucklor maintained that bunnies are meant for the dinner table, while others argued for ethical treatment of these animals in-game.

Vegetable Goblin and others, who previously had to reset their game multiple times, were involved in the discussion but kept their opinions civil despite the ribbing from others. They even offered a humorous suggestion for the developer to fix the tutorial.

Overall, the chat was chaotic, filled with jokes and playful ribbing. With a few new players joining in, the community remained friendly despite the controversial topic of the day.
