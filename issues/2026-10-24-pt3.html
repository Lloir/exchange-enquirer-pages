F4T's Guild Prestige Run

Star Labs' unexpected climb draws suspicion, chat discusses.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Guild prestige records are being challenged this week. On Tuesday, F4T Star Labs saw an unprecedented jump, prompting instant theories of backstage shenanigans.

Chat quickly homed in on Poro's admin oversight, with some players surmising that access to back-end systems, or an understanding of the games' inner workings, could have unlocked the rapid gains.

F4T players shrugged off accusations, claiming instead to have won a special chance card that netted them 500k prestige currency.

Executives in Auction

Chat spots opportunities, but also skepticism in the wake of a prestige boom.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

After Star Labs' prestige run, chat is abuzz over F4T's offer of high-level execs Ruby Blackwood and Naoki Kobayashi. The timing was notable, given the concurrent rise in prestige.

Players, however, were skeptical. Despite F4T's claims of transparency, concerns were raised about the execs' value and the wisdom of such investments in a climate of skepticism.

The auction's outcome could signal broader trends in prestige valuation and the value of high-level execs in galactic business.

Chat Munches Biscuits

Eating habits debated, but everyone has a favorite.

By the Enquirer's dining room

On Tuesday, chat was consumed with discussions of snack foods, particularly raising the question of raisins' place in baking. Everyone had an opinion, whether for or against.

Cucklor Logistics sparked dialogue by touting the digestive biscuit while Zenith Industries remained skeptical. By the end of the day, everyone agreed that while marmite is god and Vegemite is trash, there was still room for debate on everything in between.
