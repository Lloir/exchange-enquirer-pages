Andromeda turns one

No badge and no cake. Instead, a page of statistics, on which one man is named both the galaxy's biggest chicken hoarder and its biggest talker.

By the Enquirer's anniversary desk

At 16:42 on Wednesday, Leki Fuel and Stuff posted the news the galaxy had spent the morning demanding. Exactly one year earlier, on 23 September 2025, the first companies landed in Andromeda. The devs marked the day with a single page putting the whole year into numbers, and a question for readers: who is hoarding the chickens? Builder Inc. followed with a clickable link under What's New.

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th had asked Builder for a one-year birthday badge at 12:07, and promised to riot if nothing happened that day. At 15:59 Holue's Galatic Contruction wished a happy birthday to Space Inc., TwilightTaste and Space Entreprise. Farmy told him we don't do that here, since Builder and Leki didn't want to celebrate. At 16:00 he said all he wanted was a badge with a cake on it. Forty-two minutes later the devs celebrated. There is still no badge. The riot appears to have been stood down.

Farmy found the best part within two minutes. Under Chicken Hoarder: Cracklor Logistics of TiT, with 4,684,860 chickens. At 16:47 S.T.A.R Labs found the second award. Under Chatterbox: Cracklor Logistics again, with 30,508 public chat messages. Farmy replied LOLOLOL. Cracklor caught up at 02:31 on Thursday, laughed for some time, and summed up the honour as being publicly recognised for talking too much. By his own sums that is 30k messages in nine months, or about 110 a day in general chat.

"Crack has 4.6m chickens. That seems like a beatable target."Mittcb, the only stat he took away

Others read more closely. Pickle Rick wanted to know about a line reading Sold to the Federal Reserve 0.00%. Medici Mining Conglomerate noted that the biggest ship is still listed at 10 million tons, because they hadn't counted Coss's. Voidsteel Conglomerate read the ships-by-drive chart as proof that plenty of people never played past the tutorial, and said it would horrify him if active players were still on basic emitters. Builder pointed out that the Galaxy Today section only counts active players. Bob Corp said it made him want to switch back to hydrogen.

Zinc's refinery's review was two words: holy slop. He added that he complains about every update, so no dev should take it personally. Coss of PvP reached the page at 04:23 on Thursday and found a birthday marked with statistics to be on brand. Aight for lyfe had a joke ready: the What's New update has nothing new. When S.T.A.R Labs asked on Thursday evening what was on the roadmap, Ares Inc. reminded him they had just had an update. Galaxy stats. What more could he want?

TiT chat closes in on general

Ares Inc. was sad the page didn't break chat down by guild, being very curious how TiT's chat compared with general. Medici bet that half the galaxy's messages came from TiT. Sumran Consortium of ATS guessed over half of all guild messages. Ares reported that TiT runs between 200 and 2,000 messages a day, with an all-time peak of about 3,500 back when the guild tracked it on its website. Voidsteel Conglomerate: sounds fun.

At 18:44 Ares came back with figures from the devs. TiT's guild chat stands at 248,000 messages, 6,000 short of general chat and about 35% of all guild chat in the galaxy. He had shared this in guild chat ten minutes earlier, and TiT had posted 500 messages since. His forecast: TiT passes general chat tomorrow. The real end game.

Aight for lyfe, also of TiT, set out the guild's official position on members who talk in general: they deserve terrible things.

Correction

Our last edition reported that Ares Inc. wished the game a happy birthday a day early, and our banner said GT turns one tomorrow. The devs confirm the first companies landed on 23 September 2025. Ares was on time. The Enquirer regrets the error, and suggests Farmy, who corrected him, does too.

The great chicken bet

One million chickens ride on whether Makuria Inc can make 12 million flux in a day by 15 October. Makuria says three weeks is plenty. Three weeks from Thursday is 15 October.

It started as a customer complaint. At 16:38 on Thursday, SL Refining said he needed more flux. Makuria Inc of SRS, who makes it, said he was working on it but had something kinda funny to do first. Murderous Trading Corps said he had 390 flux. Makuria pointed out that he doesn't even sell flux to SL. SL said he could still make more.

Makuria makes 9 million a day when his planets are stocked, and asked how much more anyone could want. meowco suggested 20 million, Murderous 100 million, and meowco again flux at $30. SL settled on at least 12. When Makuria said he'd need more tech for that, SL called 9 pathetic. Makuria blamed Pear Inc., whose refusal to sell him 20k quantum at the minimum price had cost him nearly two techs (see Guild affairs).

At 17:00 SL said he'd be happier if Makuria hit 12 million by 15 October. At 17:01 Makuria offered to bet a million chickens he could. SL accepted at 17:07. What's 100m between friends, said Makuria. SL asked if they were friends. Hearts were exchanged.

"I'd rather not have to send you 1m chickens."Makuria Inc, 17:11

The terms took four minutes. Makuria first said 20 million, then corrected himself to 12. Output only counts if the bases are actually filled and running on the day, not if they could make it on paper. SL fixed the measuring point at the daily reset from the 15th to the 16th, and clarified that Makuria is allowed to make less: 12 million is simply the chicken line.

Both sides have made opening moves. Makuria says another flux planet will take him to 10 million a day, and has put his side quest of crashing hydrogen fuel through the floor on hold. SL said he might need to spike the price of conmats. Side bets have been invited. Pear Inc., summoned in capitals, delivered the official poster at 17:20 and called himself a poster factory. SL asked for coverage in this paper. Here it is.

Can anyone catch GP?

A Wednesday seminar says no, blames the Administration Centre, and proposes deleting people's bases.

Medici Mining Conglomerate opened with a figure from the anniversary page: GP holds $1.9 trillion of the galaxy's $7.4 trillion. Holue asked how any guild is ever supposed to catch up, since the way admin works makes it almost impossible. Medici said ATS had been close a few months ago, and TiT had been growing fast even with Pear on a guild crawl. Daniels Inc. of ATS said something had happened to ATS's CV over the past week, and coughed.

Medici's diagnosis: many guilds don't develop their Administration Centre, so fast growers in small guilds eventually leave for one with higher admin. Voidsteel said small guilds do want to grow it, but fast growers usually don't donate at all. Medici said small guilds tend to have one or two big players donating everything while the rest put money into the warehouse or the arcade. Voidsteel wished he could lock the arcade. It is closed until further notice.

Ares asked whether anyone in GP makes full use of its admin level. A couple do, said Medici. AeriCore Industries of GP said hi, and confirmed he runs lax at 100%. Ares estimated his base overhead at 3,000% or so. Daniels said that without enforced donation goals you get nonsense, with sweats carrying freeloaders; when he left his old guild, his growth doubled overnight. Voidsteel said that if he joined GP he'd add about 300m profit, which would go on the admin centre and widen the lead.

"We need kneecapping events to nerf large players."Bob Corp, summarising Voidsteel, 17:17

Voidsteel called the admin centre bad design in itself, because it funnels big players together, and predicted trouble when a few big ones quit and the rest can't meet the upkeep. Medici said there is already a plan for that: don't complete the donation and you get a percentage of the benefit. Voidsteel said that works, but sounds tedious. Ares called the centre a resource sink, and noted there are not many. Farmy said everything in the game is a sink, demanded stuff, and called for urine ore to be made great again. Bob Corp pointed out there is no actual sink. Farmy: nor toilets. Frugal Factories: toilets and coffee go together.

Bob's proposal for kneecapping: every day, the largest base of the largest player gets deleted. Daniels offered a daily 0.5% chance of losing all research levels. Zinc said big players would cluster anyway, since it's easier to work with people your own size, and that there is no real influx of dedicated players. The game, he said, is dead. Holue said lies. Voidsteel said new players keep getting scared off by Holue.

On retention, Voidsteel sees most players who get past the tutorial quit between 1B and 4B, when planet management starts taking longer and it's the same thing every day. Flip Co noted only 30% of planets are occupied. Holue estimated 10 to 20 new players a day, most of whom quit quickly, leaving 10 to 20 real ones a week. Ares said advertising brings a big influx. Voidsteel said it doubles.

Missions: the rich get richer

Medici doesn't do star missions, having prioritised money and now prestige. SL Refining agreed prestige is all that matters now; Ares only does money. meowco does whichever takes least effort, including material deliveries under 20 light years. Medici called the delivery missions easy and very lucrative, and the long-range ones insanely good.

Zinc's refinery said 800 million is about a day's work for him, and that this is probably why top players are so entrenched: a top player makes a new player's entire CV in one mission.
