Squads Battle it Out

TiT and crew fight for dominance in a chaotic 4-player chess match.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

In a match of wits and patience, TiT and friends nearly lost it all to sheer frustration.

From the moment F4T proclaimed the site hateful, the team wrestled against their own confusion.

As the night wore on, the game remained stuck in 'the lobby', leaving everyone on edge.

When it all finally came together just before daily reset, it felt like a victory in and of itself.

But wait, what's this? Some poor sap couldn't figure out how to play.

The Enquirer can't help but chuckle: the perfect end to a night filled with strategic battles and technical trials.
