Pulse of the Galaxy

Sleepy morning, chicken-mad afternoon

The day opened on a whimper. Farmy mourned that there were hardly any messages overnight, S.T.A.R Labs called it awfully quiet, and someone checked the game for a pulse. Several players also announced how broke they were, from M'kauria's 1.3m down to Red Dwarf's 3,985, which in this paper's experience is how people start a conversation when there's nothing else to say.

Then the afternoon took off, and almost all of it ran on poultry. There was the flux-for-chickens wager, Kentaurus naming a Ship's Chicken on every vessel, Red Dwarf discovering he couldn't sell his chickens, and Ares's whole investment advice being 'Chickens stonks'. Under the clucking there was some real market grumbling too, about vanishing emitters, a 5m-ton special delivery and a lot of REX going up. Mood: idle, a bit giddy, and keeping a close eye on prices.

UNRESOLVED

FLUX IT! M'KAURIA BETS THE HENHOUSE ON 12 MILLION

The self-styled Flux King has one day to hit 12m flux or hand over a million chickens, and he is still a few hundred thousand short.

By the Enquirer's poultry futures desk

M'kauria Flux Consortium has put his chickens where his mouth is. In his words, he has to produce 12m or more flux tomorrow or he owes SL a million chickens. His best day so far, he told chat, was 11.5m. The other side of the bet pointed out that 11.5 isn't 12 and has already started building a home for the birds, which the Enquirer thinks is either confidence or very good carpentry.

ROI only matters if you plan on quitting

M'kauria Flux Consortium

M'kauria says he has 12.2m of flux production potential and nothing to distract him from filling every flux base all day. That is a margin of 200k, about the width of a chicken feather. His dates were also a bit fuzzy: he said the window was the 15th to the 16th and that it was 'still the 14th', while our log is stamped the 13th. Whose calendar is right, we leave to the bookmakers.

In between, he kept talking business. He said flux shouldn't be above $60 'for everyone', offered TiT a special rate of 100 (generous, if you're him), and summed up his whole philosophy in one line. Ares, meanwhile, shouted that TiT would take in the Flux King, only to then call him too small and tell him to keep growing. Lovely recruitment pitch, that.

Not all of his advice went down well. He told new players to fit heavy heat and radiation shielding on both starter ships on day one, and Zenith industries told him to stop gaslighting the new players. M'kauria said he couldn't be blamed if people followed advice with the same ROI as the devs' update cycle. Whether the chickens end up changing hands, this paper will find out tomorrow, the same time as everyone else.

ALLEGEDLY

CLOUD JAILED AGAIN AS CHAT PLAYS DETECTIVE

Cloud Refineries wants to know who reported him. Nobody's owning up.

By our man outside the cells

Cloud Refineries turned up asking whether Pear had reported him. Pear denied it, said all he'd seen was a DM from Cloud that got deleted and then Cloud in jail, and admitted he'd simply assumed Cloud had been spamming. That is the level of evidence chat usually works on, so nobody batted an eyelid.

Later Cracklor asked what Cloud had actually done, and Pear said he was advertising his game. The Enquirer can't confirm that and isn't going to try. Berlin food distributions managed a startled 'Cloud is back??', and Cloud said something rude about the devs, which this family newspaper won't print.

Drone inc. was quick to invite Cloud to PvP, noting that playing is not required and that his personality would be a great fit. That's either a warm welcome or a very neat insult, and we think Drone knew exactly which.

UNPROVEN

LIN GAO AUCTION HITS 983M AS BOOSTING CLAIMS FLY

Zinc points a finger, Cracklor answers with heavy sarcasm.

By the Enquirer's auction house correspondent

Pevilot SA reported that Lin Gao was 'getting hard', with the current price at 983,249,577 after 43 bids. That's a lot of zeroes for one name on a list.

sumran "i offer boosh prices to every newbie" is boosting, i dont beleive it

Cracklor Logistics.

Zinc's refinery then said he thought Sumran was boosting. Cracklor replied with what this paper reads as heavy sarcasm, which we're printing below rather than trying to explain. Nothing in the log confirms any wrongdoing. Ares just said 'OH NO', which is fair.

Elsewhere, RNK's Sparky explained that the guild's pie machine is not a metaphor: they turn people into pies, and he says that counts as recycling. Murderous Trading Corps was demanding sacrifices for it in capitals. Bob Corp said 'soylent green', and our food critic has asked for the day off.

WHERE HAVE ALL THE EMITTERS GONE?

Quantum FTL Emitters run dry and a spreadsheet goes into the red.

By the Enquirer's supply chain desk

Skid-Inc found out the hard way why his spreadsheet showed Quantum FTL Emitters at minus 28K profit: the market had run out. Berlin food distributions suggested setting the price to max_price whenever quantity hits zero. Skid said the API has no max_price, so the cell will turn gold instead. Turning a supply crisis into colour-coding is the most spreadsheet-player fix we've ever seen.

People making Repair Kits ate them all

Skid-Inc

225 Manufactura wanted to know where the emitters were, with four crying emojis, and asked whether people could be cut to get them back. Quiet Berm Solutions was surprised anyone uses them at all. Skid explained they can be the cheapest way to make Repair Kits, said Q4 drives are the only drive anyone should be using, and pointed to Extra-dimensional FTL Emitters for actual ships.

Skid is now thinking about doubling his Micronics Factory planet from 250 to 500 sooner than planned, since things look under-produced across the board. Other worries from the day: Medici Mining Conglomerate said special delivery is now 5m tons and the ship needs upgrading again, and Ares noted a lot of REX going up.

NEWCOMER ASKS WHERE TO PUT 4 MILLION. CHAT SAYS CHICKENS

A sensible question gets the advice it deserved, and some it didn't.

By our personal finance pages

Lektronic Industries asked a perfectly reasonable question: what's a good way to invest 4 million? Dumb said buy a house. Lektronic explained he meant spacebucks, and FastLions came back with a Construction Kit. Ares Inc. said 'Chickens stonks', and Cracklor agreed chickens are good at the moment. That's the whole consultation.

There was some genuine good news. Make Stuff INC announced it has grown from a small company to a medium one, and Cracklor marked the occasion with a chicken per million. Over on the welding desk, GreaVroes LLC asked why Truss weighs more than the parts that go into it. Void LLC said magic, GreaVroes guessed it works like burning steel wool, and Cracklor's theory involved smuggling, which we're treating as a joke.

Exchange weather

Heavy chicken showers all day, clearing to sunny spells for anyone holding poultry. A dry patch over the Quantum FTL Emitter market is expected to last, with spreadsheet cells turning gold. Flux pressure builds overnight ahead of a 12m front tomorrow.

ALDERA PRESIDENCY UP FOR GRABS IN BIZARRE BOARDROOM DRAMA

With the president out of action, five candidates line up and one drops out by teatime.

By the Enquirer's political desk (fictional elections only)

aldera mining company posted a very dramatic in-character bulletin: company president Hale Emerson had come to a grim end and could no longer serve. We've left out the gory details. M'kauria suggested calling Dr House. Farmy helped out by posting aldera's full sales list, eleven metals from Iron to Tiridium Alloy, which is either loyalty or free advertising.

aldera then named the candidates: Nao Ito, François Fontaine, Sébastien Deschamps, Blake Sterling and Vega Karr. MuFFes thought it was funny, and Farmy spotted that there are two Nao Itos. By 16:16 Fontaine had joined a coalition under Deschamps and pulled out of the race.

Holue's Galatic Contruction then gave his view on Deschamps and Mbappe, and we suspect he was talking about a different contest altogether. Entropy said he'd played BioShock and doesn't trust Fontaines. The election is still unresolved, and the Enquirer endorses nobody.

SHIP'S CHICKENS, LOBSTER LOBBY AND A RECRUITING WAKE

The smaller stories from a busy afternoon.

By the society column

Kentaurus Ballistics has decreed that every one of its ships gets a Ship's Chicken as official mascot. That's only five ships so far, as Cracklor pointed out, though Kentaurus says it's working on it. Gundercorp argued that lobsters are better as pets and as food, and was ignored.

Holue's Galatic Contruction told Zenith industries he'd tried to recruit him into FTU when he joined. Zenith was not kind about FTU, and Holue's admitted he'd given up recruiting for them. Bob Corp said RIP to his old guilds, EMS among them, and Holue's said he loves EMS. Yum Yum Chems promised Holue's he'll be back at swarm eventually.

S.T.A.R Labs is talking about hosting a race when he reaches 10 billion, with 100k, 75k and 50k Vitaqua for the top three. Banana asked whether that was next year's plan, which was cruel and possibly accurate.
