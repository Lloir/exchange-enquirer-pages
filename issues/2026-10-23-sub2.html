Old Hands, New Tricks

Seasoned veterans and eager newcomers share their secrets.

By the Enquirer's career desk

From late nights in call centers to long walks in the countryside, Cucklor Logistics and Medici Mining Conglomerate traded life stories with the younger crowd. Cucklor’s rags-to-riches tale highlighted the importance of resilience and adaptability, while Medici looked back on the halcyon days of 2010.

Cucklor reminisced about learning the ropes from the ground up, starting temp jobs and evolving into a key player with grit and determination. 'I started at the bottom and worked my way up,' he said.

Meanwhile, Medici shared memories of a time when startup dreams were still within reach, and 'life wasn’t as stressful.' The chat buzzed with stories of learning from experience, taking risks, and coming out on top.

In a candid moment, Cucklor revealed, 'I wish I had started earlier with my financial education, but once I did, it got much easier.'

Generational Divide

Millennials and Gen Z debate the challenges of today’s job market.

By the Enquirer's society desk

The chat buzzed with discussions between younger players about their struggles in the modern job market. Zinc's refinery, at just 22, contrasted the opportunities and setbacks he faces compared to the generations before him.

Zink quipped, 'I wish I was born 20 years ago when there were better opportunities,' reflecting a sentiment shared by many of his peers. 'The current job market is overwhelming.'

In response, Cucklor, with nearly two decades more experience, had a different take. 'Depends on your moxie,' he shot back. 'No one says it’s easy, but there's always a way to turn things around.'
