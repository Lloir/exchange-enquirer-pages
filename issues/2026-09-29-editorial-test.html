PEAR-ANORMAL ACTIVITY: FRUIT FLIPPED BACK FOR ANOTHER WEEK

Barely a day after joining ATS, Pear is traded to PvP for Future Considerations and left, unconscious, on the doorstep.

By the Enquirer's transfer window desk

Readers of our last issue will remember Pear being loaned out like a library book. Well, the library has a returns policy. Pear opened the day asking ATS to accept his application because he was in full production mode and needed an admin centre, and assured chat it was only for a day or so. When Farmy asked if RNK's prodigal fruit was coming home, Pear replied simply: ATS first. The Enquirer admires a man with an itinerary.

We are willing to entertain a 7-day trade for Future Considerations

Echelon Interstellar Group

By the afternoon ATS had apparently grown attached. Echelon Interstellar Group announced they were keeping the Pear, then posted an actual spreadsheet formula that returns CRITICAL ERROR: PEAR MISSING if Pear goes missing. Pear, we were told, has so many outputs. This is the most romantic thing anyone in ATS has ever said, and it was a COUNTIF.

When Farmy asked how long ATS planned to hold Pear hostage, Echelon insisted he was free to go at any time, before revising the story: Pear had not been absorbing the wisdom of the spreadsheet halls, he was intoxicated and looking for the loo. SL Refining and Farmy then offered some unflattering historical testimony on that front, and Echelon noted this had not been disclosed on the 17 page application form. The Enquirer will draw a discreet curtain over the rest.

Then, at 18:10, Coss made his move: he would like to buy Pear back for a week, please. Echelon offered a 7-day trade for Future Considerations, Coss accepted on behalf of PvP, and ATS, unable to wake the asset, called security and left him on the front steps of the PvP hideout. Coss then claimed Echelon himself was being thrown in on a 2 for 1 as reparations for emotional trauma, an arrangement Echelon seemed happy to accept provided cookies were involved.

Whether Pear has noticed any of this remains unresolved. His only other notable contribution was revealing late at night that ATS really stands for Andromeda Tuxedo Society, which, frankly, explains the application form.

The Enquirer Says

THE ENQUIRER SAYS: PEAR IS NOT A COMMODITY (YET)

When Mittcb asked when Pear became a commodity to be traded, Coss answered: as of right now. This newspaper would like to lodge a formal objection, chiefly on the grounds that if Pear is going to be traded weekly we want him listed on the exchange so we can report a proper price.

Two issues running, the galaxy's most mobile fruit has changed hands on a handshake and a promise of Future Considerations. Nobody has said what those considerations are. Nobody has asked Pear. Pear, to be fair, was asleep in the knowledge archives. We urge all parties to sort their paperwork out, add an 18th page, and at the very least wake the player before shipping him.

— The Editor

For the avoidance of doubt, 'Future Considerations' is not a new commodity on the exchange, however much the Pear market might need one.

BILLION-A-DAY BRAG-OFF: 1B DECLARED 'TRASH'

Tycoons compare their best days, and one poor soul's billion gets binned.

By our man with a very large calculator

Coss told chat he had made 1B that day, which in most walks of life would call for champagne. Cracklor ruled that for Coss's CV it was trash, and adding that he himself was making 2b a day at that size. Coss did not fight it, admitting he missed his 3.5 to 4 a day. We have seen more fight in a sleeping Pear.

Ares Inc. offered a one-day record of 2.5, said metals never gave those sorts of margins, and then quietly let slip he was on +2.2B at the moment and would hit 100 billion tomorrow. Coss congratulated him, Echelon invited him into the 100B club, and the Enquirer noted the classic Ares move of being humble and then being loaded.

For the record board: Coss says his best was just over 4B, from his electronics days, and Echelon claims a best CV day of +$4.5B. Meanwhile Coss says he is donating so much he will soon be under 100B and going backwards. Our sympathy is available, but only in small amounts.

IRON PRICES SPARK UNHOLY DEMANDS FROM NAGASH

Plus eight thousand prefabs go begging and a newcomer pitches bonds.

By the Enquirer's commodities correspondent

Such is the supply of Nagash wanted to know why iron is so expensive, and received the only answer the name allowed: Drone inc. replied, such is the demand of Nagash. S.T.A.R Labs had been about to ask the same thing. Nagash decreed thou shalt produce more, and the conversation drifted towards child labour law, where Drone inc. clarified it is apparently fine in metallurgy but verboten in mining. The Enquirer notes nobody actually produced more iron.

Kodiak Raven Inc asked if anyone wanted to buy 8,367 prefabs, and then deleted the message. Either someone bought them very quickly or somebody had second thoughts. The log does not say which, and we are not guessing.

V.O.C brought an idea from Sim Companies: a bond system letting rich players lend to starting players at interest, arguing it would help liquidity without extra admin overhead. Echelon's response was that if you gave him debt to play with he would be three times as big, which is exactly the kind of endorsement that gets proposals quietly shelved. Whether anyone has suggested it to the devs remains unanswered.

Exchange weather

Iron is running hot today, with Nagash and S.T.A.R Labs both asking why it costs so much. Drinking Water stocks continue to evaporate: Echelon reports his stack shrank from over 600M to 179,375,448 because the galaxy is too thirsty. Polyethylene is building up fast, with Coss sitting on 64,368,408 and chasing half of all production, though aparty points out it is already at fed cap.

COOKIE MONSTERS: BAKE-OFF DESCENDS INTO CHICKEN BEHAVIOUR

Echelon was promised cookies. He got cookies. He also may now be a chicken.

By the Enquirer's pastry and poultry desk

Cookies became the currency of the evening. Berlin food distributions shared a recipe review (soft, chocolate, walnuts, raisins), Voidsteel Conglomerate insisted cookies should not be soft, and Berlin conceded maybe they needed longer. They have since been eaten, which is the only review that matters.

Mittcb announced he had sent Echelon a cookie, which turned out to be one Silicon Wafer, as the trade log helpfully confirmed. He sent Zenith one too. The Enquirer applauds anyone who can pass off electronics as baked goods.

Then Drone inc. declared herself the true ATS cookie baker, members only, sorry Skye Blu Foods, and mentioned a slight chance her hallucinogenics had got into the batter and the chicken feed. Echelon reported fuzzy legs and a strange urge to peck Grain, Farmy diagnosed him as a chicken, and Drone suggested he hop over to PvP. Whether the entire guild is now on a poultry diet is unresolved.

NEWCOMER CORNER: FERTILITY, FIRINGS AND 400M

Chat does its best impression of a helpful community, with mixed results.

By the Enquirer's welcome wagon

Sternenblume arrived confused, making only concrete and prefabs, and asked what else to do. YoYo Ltd. advised buying, producing, selling and scaling up, which is the whole game in eleven words, and Makuria offered to buy the prefabs on Aetheria 8. Lek then explained that the good fertile worlds are out on the rim and only a handful of players farm, and Sternenblume learned that fertility can go over 100. A genuinely useful exchange, and the Enquirer is as surprised as you.

AMY celebrated 400m CV, earning a woot from Unicorn Industries and congratulations from Makuria. Voidsteel said hitting a billion comes with free Chickens, which Unicorn had never heard of. Skye Blu Foods spent a fair chunk of the afternoon pestering SL Refining for this free chicken, and SL declared it not his problem. We are withholding judgement on the chicken policy.

Frugal Factories, meanwhile, earned the achievement nobody wants: his solo guild's Administration Center is now too powerful for his workforce, helped along by firing all his technicians, and so many staff that his next mission was a Food Shipment to his old base. Makuria asked if he had considered hiring more people. He says probably, once the fleet reaches the rim. Late on, mibaelscopic scientific inc asked what planet tiers do, and as of going to press, chat never got round to answering.
