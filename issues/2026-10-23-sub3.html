Rabbits Under Siege!

GreaVroes LLC and STAR Labs duke it out over the true state of matter and morality.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

General chat was the sight of a heated dispute between GreaVroes LLC's Cucklor and STAR Labs' labs as they argued whether rabbits are rocks or culinary delicacies. Cucklor, championing rabbit rights, declared rabbits fluffy citizens deserving of freedom and cuddles. Labs, however, insisted they were dinner and even shared recipes for rabbit curries.

Vegetable Goblin tried to lighten the mood but was met with heated rebuttals. 'No, you can’t eat my friends!' Cucklor exclaimed, while Labs doubled down, 'Come to HQ, they’re every day.'

The Enquirer is shocked by the intensity. Rabbit rights activists and culinary enthusiasts alike took sides, turning the chat into a delightful mess of opinions and recipes. It’s time to choose: cuddle the bunnies or cook them up!

But who knew the rabbit stew was boiling over, metaphorically speaking?

The Great Debate Over Rocks

Heisenberg and Scam battle the definition of a rock while Bob Corp observes.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Heisenberg and Scam embarked on a philosophical journey to define what constitutes a rock. Scam contested, 'How tf is Carbon a rock! The game is lying to you.' Heisenberg, however, insisted 'It’s a mineral, I leave it to Bob Corp's expertise.'

Scam pushed back with, 'You can’t make minerals by burning wood,' while Heisenberg offered, 'Ok, then die.' In a moment of comedic relief, Heisenberg offered, 'I smell glu' while Scam had a field day, 'Lmao, Star not the time.'

The Enquirer can't help but chuckle at the absurdity of it all, but it's still unclear if we're dealing with hard rock or something entirely softer.
