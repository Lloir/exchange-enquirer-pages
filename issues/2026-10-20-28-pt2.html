Rabbit Frenzy Grips TiT

Vegetable Goblin's rabbit antics leave the server in tatters.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Vegetable Goblin brought the house down with an ongoing rabbit discussion turning into a frantic battle over the critters' culinary fate. F4TZenith industries even pitched in, insisting that rabbits are best served in a stew. Krijer Industries attempted to defuse the situation by invoking rabbit privacy but was promptly ignored.

The rabbit debate escalated to absurd claims about butter being grey, dandelions being yellow, and farmy eating crayons.

Despite all this, the general consensus remained clear: rabbits are for cuddling, not cooking. Mental Rabbit, a vocal defender of rabbits’ rights, took to the chat to emphasize the importance of keeping rabbits out of it all. A few players still clung to hope, with Berlin Food Distributions offering a final salvo at the rabbit conundrum’s end.

In the midst of this, Vegetable Goblin and Heisenberg, along with a few others, began plotting their next Monopoly game moves.

Metal Market Struggles

Tiridium Alloy's overproduction risks crashing.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Faced with a flood of Tiridium Alloy on the market, Ares Inc. fears potential crashes. The market was oversaturated, leading to rampant undercutting. Players like GPZinc's refinery were quick to note the risks and began focusing on other resources.

Concerns were further fueled by the Wall of Tiridium Alloy, highlighting the overproduction issue. Despite this, GPZinc's refinery remained optimistic, noting the potential for a shift in strategy.

Ares Inc. acknowledged the trend but was unsure of the future value of Metal products. The market continued to operate in a state of uncertainty, leading some players to consider other investments.

ALLEGEDLY

Monopoly Game Update

Players draft cards for competitive game play.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Players discussed GPZinc's refinery draft for a Monopoly game, leading to numerous debates over chance cards, property names, and game mechanics. The debate focused on property values and rules, attempting to balance them while ensuring monetary changes.

Proposed ideas were met with skepticism and a need for compromise, ensuring a fun yet balanced game for players. A few players attempted to clone the game source or navigate GitHub, but ultimately, the draft relied on Excel sheets for balance.

In the end, the Monopoly draft was a community effort, blending creativity with simplicity, to ensure a fair game for all players.
