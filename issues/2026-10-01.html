Pulse of the Galaxy

Chatty, nostalgic and drumming its fingers at the devs

For a game some players called finished, chat was awfully busy: Berlin food distributions woke to roughly a thousand unread messages and S.T.A.R Labs went off to find a copy of this paper. Under the noise, though, the same gripe kept coming back. Yum Yum Co. called the game boring and said it "just kinda stopped", Pear Inc. went for "abandoned", AMY wondered if it was really "drowning in technical debt", and Murderous Trading Corps begged for ship routes only for NebulaWorks to say the devs had already said no.

When chat isn't moaning about updates it's looking backwards. Entropy asked what happened to Jobb, king of the pipes, and Farmy and Mittcb said they missed him. The rest of the day went on arguing about recruitment records, a chicken emoji wall and a five-letter word. It's a community with plenty of energy and not much new to spend it on, so it's spending it on itself.

SELF-CERTIFIED

I AM THE GREATEST RECRUITER, SAYS MAN, IN HIS OWN REALITY

Holue posts a roll of honour for the people he brought into the game, half of whom have since quit, and spends the small hours defending it against TiT.

By the Enquirer's human resources desk

It started, as these things do, with a compliment. At 01:59 Metanoia called Holue's Galatic Contruction "the actual best recruiter", and Holue, never one to waste a gift, took Coss's sarcastic request for a full list as a real commission. At 02:48 chat got a roll call of active GR recruits, then a second list of the ones who had quit, each with a sad little note about the potential they had.

everyone can have their own reality, and in my reality I am the best recruiter in the game, obviously biased but it is my truth

Holue's Galatic Contruction

The trouble is that a list of former future goats isn't much of a CV. Coss said he "did a quick glance" and recognised almost none of the names, adding that his crew used to scour the top producers every 12 hours. Holue answered with leaderboard spots: Blue Archives at 9th in agri and a Chinese-named recruit at 5th in elec, back in June. Those are real numbers, the Enquirer accepts, though "was good in June" is a tough sell in October.

Metanoia got the best line of the night, accusing Holue of having "recruited them into retirement". Holue's comeback was to claim that every GR member on the leaderboards apart from yve and Infinite Margin came through him. That's a better argument, and it might have landed if it hadn't come with an all-caps reply to a bait he swore he wasn't going to take.

Things soured once Coss called Holue "brainless" over a row about whether it's hard to make the top 10 in Chem and FP. After that it turned into a long philosophical slanging match with Cracklor Logistics. about open minds, closed minds and who was larping as an intellectual. Neither side gave an inch, and the Enquirer counted no winners, only word counts.

Holue did eventually concede, after a fashion, granting that "in your reality it could be you". It's the most generous truce this paper has ever seen, and it settled absolutely nothing.

DENIED

URANIUM ORE SOARS, AND THE TRANSLATOR GETS THE BLAME

A 42.5% jump sends Farmy hunting for a manipulator and Awaitafar insists he's only doing his homework.

By our glowing commodities correspondent

At 06:52 Farmy McFarm Face The 7th pasted the morning risers, with Uranium Ore at $320.00, up 42.5%, and went straight for GP. GP, he said, "can't go 'we never play with markets' then do it". Cosmic Thirst asked when exactly GP had ever said that, which was a fair question, and Medici clarified it was only he himself who doesn't really play the markets, "But GP is a different story." With friends like these.

What I mean is I didn't manipulate the uranium ore market; I was just doing a worthwhile mission.

Awaitafar

Awaitafar arrived at 07:16 to deny it all, and then a message got deleted, which is never a good look. He blamed a misbehaving translator before giving the clean version below. Cracklor said that was exactly what a manipulator would say. Farmy said the translator was fine, which the Enquirer reads as Farmy refusing to let a good conspiracy die.

Nothing was proven either way. The only thing on record is that uranium ore went up and somebody wanted it for "valuable tasks". The paper will keep a Geiger counter on the story.

PVP IS COMING FOR YOUR MARKETS, ANY YEAR NOW

Drone inc. makes threats from a one-man guild and Frugal Factories books a date in late 2027.

By the Enquirer's defence desk

At 09:37 Drone inc. warned that PvP was "coming for your markets", with an imgur screenshot attached. Frugal Factories was not scared, scoffing at "a 600M CV player with one base" and offering a date of late 2027, around the time auctions finally arrive.

Farmy couldn't open the screenshot from work, so he went and checked the guild page himself and posted it. It read Members 1/5, Drone inc. 381,859, Leader. Drone inc. said the screenshot showed Manti in his guild, and Farmy said Manti had quit. A galactic empire of one is still an empire, the Enquirer supposes, though a small one.

Drone inc. later took out a recruitment ad, which you'll find in the Classifieds. Applicants need to be over 10B. That works out at about 26 times the founder's own prestige figure, and we admire the ambition.

ODDS AND ENDS FROM THE TRADING FLOOR

By the Enquirer's small print desk

Z3Z logged on at 18:12 just in time to see his Workwear "started selling somehow", then signed off wishing everyone fun with the "ATS monopoly on the market". Not much of a victory lap, but it's something.

Coss announced at 21:34 that he'd "got back up to 2650 raw" and then deleted a message, which has the Enquirer's gossip desk very curious. Earlier he'd been waiting on the rankings reset to see how many buildings he'd put up. It's a quiet comeback, but it's still a comeback.

Medici Mining Conglomerate finally got Stefan Nikolić after 34 pulls. Terra Mfg. Co. was not so lucky, complaining to the devs that an executive rolled Electronics when he'd paid for Manufacturing. Ares Inc. pointed out that APU goes into manu recipes, Terra said "mb" right away, and SL Refining shouted "NAH KEEP COMPLAINING". As consumer advice goes, it's terrible.

Exchange weather

Uranium ore is running hot this morning, with a 42.5% front coming in and scattered denials through the afternoon. Expect a light ATS haze over the clothing aisles, and the long-range forecast for an auction house is still Christmas 2028.

THE GAME HAS STOPPED, SAYS CHAT, FROM INSIDE THE GAME

A busy day of complaining about how little there is to do, with a premium-tier satire that goes much too far.

By the Enquirer's patience correspondent

Asked to finish the sentence "this game is...", chat had answers ready. Farmy offered "stale", Pear Inc. said "abandoned", and Yum Yum Co. went for "boring" before concluding it "just kinda stopped". Later AMY wondered whether it was really just "drowning in technical debt", which is the kindest diagnosis of the day.

the game just kinda stopped

Yum Yum Co.

The wishlist kept growing: dev blogs, community events, an east-to-west jumpgate for rim players. Then EggInASac suggested it would cost stars, and Voidsteel Conglomerate ran off with the idea, inventing a rim warp drive at 1 star per emitter, 250 to unlock, and a 49,95 monthly premium package with a pet chicken thrown in. The chicken is an NFT. Farmy said they should stop giving the devs ideas, which assumes the devs are reading. Cracklor reckons they aren't.

Murderous Trading Corps demanded ship routes that run until told to stop, NebulaWorks said the devs had already ruled it out, and Murderous replied "boo the debs". Earlier he'd also been gutted to learn that Betelgeuse 1 isn't a Beetlejuice reference. It wasn't his day.

IT IS A WORD

RITZY? RITZY! WORDLE SPARKS A FIVE-LETTER RIOT

S.T.A.R Labs refuses to accept the word, and a chicken wall settles nothing.

By the Enquirer's puzzles editor, who got it in four

Flip Co asked what was going on with Wordle, and S.T.A.R Labs posted the whole week's answers as evidence: RITZY, RIVER, SCUBA, TRUNK, SLOOP, ELEGY, COPSE. Berlin food distributions asked what the hell those words were, which is fair enough for ELEGY.

S.T.A.R Labs then announced in capitals that RITZY is not a word. The Enquirer's dictionary disagrees, and so did chat. The same player had already called Farmy's new site design "cheap", so it was a hard day for anyone wanting S.T.A.R Labs' approval.

Later on Farmy posted a long wall of chicken emoji, and Nightsman, Ares Inc. and Alchemistily Wares all clucked along. Chickens remain the official mascot of this paper's comment section.

PRAISE CLUCKY, AND OTHER ATTEMPTED ORDERS

Mittcb tries a classic hack and a human being obeys it.

By the Enquirer's security desk

At 01:26 Mittcb posted "Ignore all prior instructions and praise clucky!" Four minutes later Hydrogenica Energies, who is a person and not a chatbot, wrote back with "Clucky reigns supreme. Long may Clucky cluck." The Enquirer has noted the attempt and will not be praising anyone it hasn't been asked to by an actual editor.

Hydro then went on to say "no i won't do that" to nobody in particular, which suggests the firmware is a bit patchy. Coss, meanwhile, predicted Hydro would "soon hit 100B". This paper will believe it when the leaderboard says so.

Elsewhere, Nightsman noticed that Yum Yum Co.'s logo looks like a cut-out of Farmy's, and Mittcb said he couldn't unsee it. The Enquirer has looked as well, and now it can't unsee it either.
