PEAR-FECT DEAL: ATS FLOGS PEAR FOR A WEEK

He joined for an admin centre, got plugged into the spreadsheets, and ended up dumped on the PvP doorstep in a 7-day swap for Future Considerations.

By the Enquirer's transfer window desk

The morning began innocently. At 09:33 Pear Inc. asked whether anyone in ATS could accept his application, because he was "in full production mode" and needed an admin centre. Sumran Consortium said he couldn't, and explained that only the "spreadsheet officers" were online, and "they dont like fun". Pear said everyone understood it was "only for a day or so".

=IF(COUNTIF(ATS,"Pear")=1,"KEEP PEAR","CRITICAL ERROR: PEAR MISSING")

Echelon Interstellar Group

By the afternoon Pear was wearing an ATS tag and the spreadsheet officers had clearly grown attached. Echelon Interstellar Group announced "We're keeping the Pear", backed it up with a working Excel formula, and reported that Pear had been plugged into the sheets and "has so many outputs".

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th asked how long ATS planned to hold Pear hostage. Echelon insisted Pear was free to leave any time but was busy soaking up the wisdom of the halls, then admitted Pear might just have been wandering about looking for the loo. What followed was a long and frankly unprintable discussion of what should go on page 18 of ATS's already 17-page application form. The Enquirer notes only that the paper procurement department is carrying out a full audit.

Then Coss made a move: "I'd like to buy pear back for a week please." Mittcb asked when Pear had become a tradeable commodity. "As of right now," said Coss. Echelon offered a 7-day trade for Future Considerations, and Coss accepted on behalf of PvP. Coss then tried to add Echelon to the deal as a 2-for-1 to cover the "emotional trauma ATS caused me". Echelon's only question was whether there would be cookies.

Pear could not be woken for the handover. By 18:19 Echelon confirmed ATS had "left the pear on the front steps of the PvP hideout". Whether the Future Considerations ever arrive is, as of press time, unresolved.

TiT's recruitment ads are working, says TiT, holding up a quote nobody else can check.

By our fact-checking department (currently KOICRAZY)

At 10:09 Farmy McFarm Face The 7th posted what looked like a quoted ATS message from Lek, timestamped 11:05, that read simply: "I will join TIT". Farmy then declared it "KOIFIRMED" several times, in capitals.

Lek tell me its not true

Makuria Inc

Makuria Inc begged Lek to deny it, then called Farmy "KOICRAZY". Fizzy Pop Inc offered "Koiracy", and Farmy came back with "Koispiracy". Makuria gave in and said "looks like the ads worked farmy".

In the same genre, Pear Inc. then posted a quote of Farmy supposedly saying "Pear is the greatest being to have ever existed". Farmy said he stood by it.

The Enquirer notes that Lek turned up later in the day to explain fertile worlds to a newcomer, and was still being tagged as ATS. The move stays KOIFIRMED only in TiT's head.

BILLIONAIRES' ROW: THE GREAT DAILY-PROFIT BRAG-OFF

Ares Inc. is on course for the 100B club while the big hitters compare their best days.

By the Enquirer's counting-house correspondent

Late in the evening Coss said he had made 1B that day. Cracklor Logistics. said that was trash for Coss's CV, and that at the same CV he had been making 2b a day. Coss agreed it was trash and said he missed his 3.5 to 4 a day, with a record of just over 4B from his electronics run.

Ares Inc. said his one-day record was 2.5 and that he'd never had those margins in metals. Echelon Interstellar Group put in a best CV day of +$4.5B. Then Ares reported +2.2B "at the moment" and "100 bil tomorrow". Coss congratulated him and Echelon invited him into the 100B club.

Meanwhile the stockpile contest went on. Coss posted a Polyethylene stack of 64,368,408 and said he would soon have 50% of all poly production. Echelon answered with 179,375,448 Drinking Water, which he says is down from over 600M because the galaxy "sure likes to drink". aparty asked the obvious question, why poly when it's at fed cap. It went unanswered, apart from Coss saying he couldn't afford a 19B wall.

BONDS, CHICKEN BONDS: V.O.C PITCHES A LENDING MARKET

A Sim Companies veteran suggests rich players lend to newbies at interest. The rich players like the sound of debt.

By our financial innovation desk

V.O.C described a bond system from Sim Companies, where rich players lend starting players money at interest for a fixed period. They argued it would give big players returns without more admin overhead and help market liquidity, and asked whether anyone had suggested it to the devs.

The response was keen, if not exactly sober. Echelon Interstellar Group said "give me debt to play with and I'll be 3 times as big". Entropy reckoned they could have been almost 7bil CV by now.

Nobody said whether the idea has gone to the devs. The Enquirer suggests chickens as collateral.

FRUGAL FACTORIES FIRES EVERYONE, ADMIN CENTRE WINS

Cost-cutting goes a bit too well at a solo guild.

By our industrial relations desk

Frugal Factories announced the unwanted achievement of an Administration Center "too powerful for my current workforce", helped along by firing every technician. They fired so many people that the next mission was a Food Shipment to the old base.

It helps that I fired all my technicians.

Frugal Factories

Makuria Inc asked, gently, whether hiring more people had been considered. Frugal said it was "very likely" once the fleet reaches the rim. Earlier, Makuria mentioned he needs another 13 research levels, or roughly 10.3B in quant, to make consumables cheaper than a new player can. He also discovered mid-chat that he had passed 19B CV.

Exchange weather

Iron is running hot after Such is the supply of Nagash asked why it was so expensive and S.T.A.R Labs agreed. Heavy Drinking Water showers are easing as the galaxy keeps drinking, and a Polyethylene front is building fast in the PvP area. Expect 8,367 prefabs to drift through the Kodiak Raven region, followed by a sudden message deleted.

COOKIE MONSTERS: THE GREAT SOFT VERSUS CRUNCHY SCHISM

A pear trade turns into a bake-off, and one guild's cookies may have been spiked.

By the Enquirer's food critic, still chewing

Once cookies came up in the Pear negotiations, the rest of chat was hooked. Berlin food distributions said they'd made some a little over a week ago, soft, with chocolate, walnuts and raisins. Voidsteel Conglomerate ruled that cookies "shouldn't be soft though", and Berlin admitted they might have wanted a bit longer in the oven.

Mittcb handed out "cookies" to Echelon and Zenith industries. Echelon's trade log showed that the cookie was in fact 1 Silicon Wafer. Zenith said "Yummy" anyway.

Drone inc. then revealed they make ATS's cookies, for guild members only, and offered Skye Blu Foods a chicken as a consolation. Drone added that some hallucinogenics might have got into the cookie batter or the chicken feed. Soon afterwards Echelon reported fuzzy legs and "a strange need to go and peck Grain". Farmy's diagnosis: "your a chicken".

In other conmat news, Mittcb sent everything needed for a new base except one conmat, which is sitting in the exchange warehouse because the "buy to ship" button got switched off. Berlin said it happens to them too.

WELCOME, STERNENBLUME: CONCRETE, PREFABS AND FERTILITY OVER 100

A newcomer asks sensible questions and chat mostly answers them.

By our newcomers' desk

Sternenblume came in at 12:25 with a fair question: all they were doing was producing concrete and prefabs, so what else should they be doing? Makuria Inc offered to buy the prefabs on Aetheria 8. YoYo Ltd. gave the classic advice: keep buying, producing and selling, and scale up.

Next question: why are there so few companies on fertile worlds? Lek explained that the good fertile worlds are out in the rim and only a handful of people farm. Sternenblume was surprised to learn fertility can go over 100. YoYo added that only certain agri products need it.

Later mibaelscopic scientific inc asked what planet tiers do. Chat was distracted at the time, and the question is still unanswered.

ALLEGEDLY

THE CHICKEN STANDARD: AMY HITS 400M CV AND ASKS ABOUT BOOSTING

Milestones, promised poultry and a joke about Cracklor that we are printing strictly as a joke.

By the Enquirer's poultry exchange

AMY celebrated reaching 400m CV. Unicorn Industries and Makuria Inc cheered, and Voidsteel Conglomerate said the next target was a first billion "so you can get some free Chickens". Unicorn wanted to know why nobody had mentioned free chickens, and Skye Blu Foods is still waiting for theirs.

AMY then asked whether players are allowed to boost each other "using chickens as our secret currency". SL Refining answered that "cracklor does it all the time". The Enquirer treats this as chat banter, not an allegation. Nothing in the log backs it up, and Metanoia has already accused Cracklor of playing "chicken tycoon" in public.

Chickens were everywhere today. Sumran Consortium was disappointed that Medici's graphic apparently named Twilight as the biggest chicken guy rather than TiT. Cracklor replied that it only counts single players, "plus he makes them".
