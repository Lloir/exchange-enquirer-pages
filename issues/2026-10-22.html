BUNNY CHECK

Bunny Boosh: The Market's Cutest Catastrophe

EggInASac's latest price drop set the galaxy abuzz.

By the Enquirer's egg desk.

It started with a tweet, innocuous at first: EggInASac announced the price drop for bunnies, the market's cuddliest commodity. Little did the rest of the galaxy suspect this would set the stage for one of the funniest trading meltdowns.

First, the memes. EggInASac himself couldn't resist: '🥚🍳🥚', a playful nod to the inevitable trade imbalance. But then came the booshers, ready with nicknames and glee. The rabbit hole was getting deeper.

Cucklor Logistics got in on the action, offering a cheeky challenge to fellow guilds: 'Eggsecute Order 66, The Cluckening'. The Enquirer couldn't help but chuckle. With the market abnormally active, it seems even the most seasoned traders couldn't resist indulging in a bit of bunny-related chaos.

As expected, the rabbit hole deepened when Void LLC dropped the official price to '66'. The resulting market frenzy was, well, entertaining. 'Rabbit Pie'? 'Internal Basting'? The chat was alive with playful puns and questionable culinary suggestions.

And let's not forget, the real drama: TiTPIFFs Company navigating construction queues to no avail, only to be derailed by the realization he needed to reset his company and start fresh. It's a tale of mistakes and redemption, of a trader facing the bunny boosh head-on.

SPECULATIVE

Cucklor's Criminal Conspiracy?

Chat speculates over Cucklor's mysterious intentions.

By the Enquirer's conspiracy desk.

While the chat is buzzing with laughter and love for bunnies, one can't help but wonder about Cucklor's intentions. The guild's latest antics have chat buzzing, from their playful challenges to the market's strange fluctuations.

When Cucklor Logistics announced the price for bunnies at '66', the market took notice. While it's possible that this was just a well-timed market boosh, the chat remains unimpressed. Accusations of boosting and manipulation still simmer beneath the surface, as guilds vie for control of the conversation space.

And let's not forget the usual suspects. Veggie Goblin remains undeterred in his quest for a better tutorial, mocking those who got it wrong. Meanwhile, Farmy's absence is felt more keenly, as chat wonders what he's up to. 'Who turned you veggie? RNKSparky's pie machine?', they joke.

Banking Scandal Uncovered?

SpinAxis Manufacturing GtmF's Money Laundering Scheme?

By the Enquirer's money desk.

In a twist of events, SpinAxis Manufacturing GtmF proposed an audacious idea: a money laundering ring with staff auctions. Chat is abuzz with the suggestion, questioning whether this is an innocent proposal or a hidden agenda to launder the market's funds?

REQUIRED

TiT Soars, Chickens in Demand

Cucklor's latest demand for tribute

By the Enquirer's tribute desk.

In a surprising twist, TiT just crossed the trillion-guild CV mark, a significant milestone indeed. But instead of celebrating quietly, the guild wasted no time in ratcheting up the pressure: send tribute in the form of chickens or titanium at min price.

The announcement left chat divided. Some praised the guild's success, while others groaned at the thought of surrendering their resources. But no one could argue with the market power Cucklor Logistics now holds, setting the tone for future exchanges.

Will the rest of the galaxy comply? Or will this newfound demand for tribute spark a wave of resistance? Chat is eagerly watching to see who'll be next to step up to the plate. TiT's latest move is a testament to the guild's growing clout, and we'll be watching closely to see how this develops.

NEWBIE FAVORITES

Newbies' FAQ: Welcome to the Galaxy

Tips for the newest traders

By the Enquirer's newbies desk.

A new player arrived recently, seeking advice on startup production and loan funding. The chat was quick to offer advice, guiding newcomers like PIFFs Company through the game's intricacies. Pear Inc. chimed in with a simple solution: 'missions are good money, do many'.

While it's tempting to dive in headfirst, PIFFs Company found himself in a construction quandary, realizing too late that the tutorials need updating. Veggie Goblin's plea for a tutorial fix resonated with many newbies struggling to navigate the game's nuances.

But the most valuable lesson came in the form of a reset recommendation. After hours of mistakes, PIFFs Company was advised to scrap his current setup and start anew. The message: 'reset and join TiT, and we will help'. It's a tough pill to swallow, but resetting early saves more harm than good.

From a bunny boosh to mission advice, the chat is a community for new traders, ready to help those taking their first steps into the galaxy.

So, what's the secret of thriving in this galaxy? Accept the bunny boosh with a smile, embrace mission opportunities, and when all else fails, reset and start fresh. After all, nobody said building an empire would be easy.
