TIT's Wanqing Campaign

TIT uses alluring pictures of wanqing to lure new players into their guild.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

TIT has stepped up their recruiting efforts, this time with some unusual tactics. The guild is using alluring pictures of wanqing across the general chat to attract new members, including an offer to visit their secret wanqing files if they join.

JOIN TIT - WE HAVE A BASEMENT WITH WANQING PICS

Heisenberg

The chatter around the Enquirer staff desk has been thick with complaints about TIT’s constant presence, each new round more inventive than the last. From their initial enthusiastic push for new recruits to their current use of wanqing enticements, the guild's methods appear to be working in attracting new members, leaving everyone else questioning the effectiveness of their own recruitment strategies—or their own sanity.

According to guild chat, TIT has also threatened to expand into rabbit ranches for their next round of recruitment tricks, with many players expressing their concerns.

F4Tscam's Conundrum

F4Tscam's donation goal is set to double, leading to tensions within their ranks.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

F4Tscam has been handed a tough challenge: if they fail to meet their newly raised donation goal, they face removal from their guild. The goal, now set to double, has stirred plenty of unrest and debate.

Guilds with such low donation goals have typically been seen as lacking discipline, prompting a reaction in F4Tscam’s direction. The situation might be even more precarious following the recent threat of a triple donation goal if a certain line is crossed.

Rabbit Markets on the Rise

Krijer Industries eyes a new venture in rabbit farming.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Krijer Industries has sparked conversations about a new business venture: rabbit farming. The player has floated the idea of including rabbits alongside chickens and cows, leading to discussions on whether to sell rabbit meat, fur, or offer breeding services.

Rangers warn that the rabbit market is heating up—either due to the appeal of rabbit products or the potential for black market demand, the former being less controversial.

Heisenberg's Mechanical Woes

Heisenberg struggles with conveyor designs for industrial sites.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

In a recent chat, Heisenberg offered insights into their job in an industrial setting. They seem to be in charge of budgeting equipment for such sites, including conveyors. However, their workload appears to be overwhelming, with a significant amount of time required to produce thorough and detailed proposals.

This has led Heisenberg to contemplate the importance of design and installation stages, wondering how the whole project remains efficient and cost-effective.
