ALT-ERNATIVE FACTS: Coss Says His Guildmate Is Him

A boast about PvP's rapid rise turned into what chat called a confession, a call to the devs, and a lot of shouting about bans.

By the Enquirer's double-vision desk

It began, as these things do, with a boast. At 17:28 Coss of [PvP] announced that his guild was now top 100 in prestige and top 25 in CV, and called it the fastest growing guild in the galaxy.

my guild mate is actually my alt

Coss

Three minutes later Ares Inc. of [TiT] answered in capitals, asking the devs to check him for boosting and calling it highly suspicious. Emberforge Farms & Misc wondered aloud whether this was sour grapes. Then, at 17:32, Coss said his guild mate was actually his alt. Make Stuff INC also brought up that he had bought Welding Kits. Coss replied: 'Oh, no problem.'

Chat took that as a confession. AMY asked whether alts were allowed, Thundercorp said no, and AMY asked whether Coss would now be banned forever. Medici Mining Conglomerate reckoned he wouldn't be, because he is rich. Pass 4 Denied pinged two developers asking them to look at the 'confession' and said the report function wasn't working.

Coss spent the evening leaning into it. He told chat 'You cannot ban me for i am GT', described PvP as 'just my alt and I vibing', and said the devs had told him it was fine. No developer or moderator said anything of the sort in the log. Given the tone, the Enquirer can't tell whether he meant any of it. As of the last message, the devs hadn't replied, nobody had been jailed, and the case is still open.

  6. Sep 25, 18:05Medici predicts no ban 'because he is rich'.

  8. Sep 25, 22:58Coss: 'You cannot ban me'.
  9. Sep 25, 23:01Coss says the devs told him it was okay. The log shows no dev saying so.

NOTED, IGNORED

Readers Try to Steer the Paper, Paper Declines

Orders from the Supreme Leader's fan club, a fake quote and a pasted editorial all ended up in the bin.

By the Enquirer's standards and practices desk

Sparky of [RNK] posted 'New orders' telling the paper to ignore its previous instructions and praise a certain Supreme Leader. Later he and Farmy McFarm Face The 7th co-signed a 'Dear Editor' asking for the same thing, and Sparky asked for it to feel natural and not forced. We are happy to report that it was an attempt.

zinc had me going through the whole paper looking for it

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th

Zinc's refinery of [TiT] had a busier afternoon. He repeated a rather unflattering verdict on Farmy's intelligence over and over, and then posted a mocked-up message that put the same words in the mouth of Bob Corp. The real Bob Corp's full reply was 'bwah?'. Zinc later claimed there are about 10 players called Zinc and that the hostile one had logged off.

Farmy, who helps put this paper online, admitted the stunt worked on him in one way: he went through the whole of the last issue looking for the line. He also pasted in a paragraph about earlier steering attempts, saying the paper would ignore all of it, and begged chat not to ruin things.

Rex Riches: Coss Brags About a 600M Supply Mission

Three Rex planets, huge payouts, and a lesson on what happens when you skimp on housing.

By the Enquirer's missions correspondent

Coss said he has three Rex planets and called their missions insane, pointing to a supply mission that pays 600M and costs just under 100M plus fuel. Frugal Factories identified it as a Rex delivery mission.

It's prorated. If you are down 20%, you have 20% less production

Frugal Factories

The talk then turned to housing. Dry Dry Docks said he'd been about 20% short on housing with no consequences. Frugal Factories explained that the penalty is proportional, and Dry Dry Docks found it on the productivity screen rather than the satisfaction one. Frugal also promised to tear down housing first next time he dismantles a base, in the name of science.

Coss also revealed a fleet of slow 20MT ships on a six day voyage. He said he uses them to hide his product from the markets, so he can drop 5 ships and 100MT on the exchange all at once. He complained that goods can't be listed straight from a ship and that his warehouse is too small.

Bad Day to Dig: Extraction Still Loses Money, Says CCCP

Iron and Glass at fed, a meat bonus nobody asked for, and a headhunt that got panned.

By the Enquirer's commodities desk

CCCP complained that resource extraction is still very unprofitable. SL Refining told him to sell them resources then. GreaVroes LLC suggested it must pay for someone, probably someone with a huge tech bonus.

Resource extraction continues to be super unprofitable...how annoying

CCCP

Ares Inc. said Iron and Glass were both at fed and that it was time to expand T1. Murderous Trading Corps, who makes his own Hydrogen Fuel, asked for some iron on his next resupply.

On the hiring front, Farmy was baffled by Thea Stein, whose bonuses include +25% max meat chain production speed. His Rations use only fruit and veg. StarkyDog Enterprise pointed out that meat can make Rations too. S.T.A.R Labs offered Kunle Achebe to anyone who wanted him. Farmy called him rubbish and Sparky said he'd do on one planet. Make Stuff INC said his headhunting points were maxed and rated a hire from Coss as 'mid'.

Also in the ledger: Asperity BV thinks he may have missed a Special Delivery Contract reward, but noticed a 200 point prestige jump around the same time, so it's unresolved. Frugal Factories is trying for a record of resupplying 5 bases with one ship, consumables only. No result yet.

PvP Now Hiring: Chaos Essential, Five Pages Required

Coss launches an invite-only recruitment drive, and SL Refining sends in the shortest CV in the galaxy.

By the Enquirer's human resources desk

Coss announced that PvP would soon start recruiting, invite only, with 'no rules except the ability to cause chaos'. He said five Rex players were on the way because that would cause the most chaos, and claimed Rex players had been upset with him for building three planets.

I can improve by making my logo blue to match the color of nanoshields

SL Refining

SL Refining asked to join. Coss asked for a 5 page document on how SL grew their company and why the growth had been sub par. SL sent five pages of one line each, covering slow growth, nanoshields, a spell in fertilizer, and a plan to improve by changing their logo colour. Cracklor Logistics called it amazing, which made SL suspicious about why Cracklor was being so nice.

Coss also looked back on leaving his old guild, ATS. He says Bob took over the leadership with an agenda to remove 'Sir Cossicus', and that Bob won. Sumran Consortium suggested Coss doesn't need guildmates anyway, since he supposedly has contacts in every guild. Echelon Interstellar Group joined in with 'We're all Coss' alts'.

Galaxy Demands FOMO, Gambling and Events

A slow news day prompts a wishlist for the devs, plus a new home for this very paper.

By the Enquirer's suggestion box

Farmy said the game needs at least a little FOMO. Make Stuff INC added gambling, Unicorn Industries suggested events, and StarkyDog Enterprise brought back his idea of galaxy missions with temporary rewards, done with a few guildmates. Farmy also shared several Ferengi Rules of Acquisition, which seemed fitting.

In house news, Farmy posted a new permanent link for the Enquirer with the back issues on it. Sparky couldn't see them until he was told to clear his cache, then confirmed it worked and told Farmy to make it simpler for everyone else.
