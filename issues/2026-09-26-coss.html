ALT-OGETHER NOW! Coss 'confession' sets general chat ablaze

PvP boss brags of lightning growth, gets accused of boosting, then says his guild mate is his alt, and chat duly loses its mind.

By the Enquirer's alternative accounts desk

It began, as these things do, with a boast. At 17:28 Coss of PvP announced that his outfit was now a top 100 guild in prestige and top 25 in CV, calling it the fastest growing guild in the galaxy, complete with a winking face.

my guild mate is actually my alt

Coss

Three minutes later Ares Inc. of TiT was on the loudhailer, demanding in capital letters that the devs check him for boosting and calling the whole thing highly suspicious. Emberforge Farms & Misc of F4T came back with a dry question about sour grapes, and Coss answered the boosting shout with the line that would define the day: his guild mate, he said, is actually his alt.

Chat took it about as seriously as you would expect. AMY of TiT asked whether alts were allowed, Thundercorp of GR told her no, and AMY wanted to know if Coss would now be banned forever for his CONFESSION. Medici Mining Conglomerate reckoned he wouldn't be, because he is rich. Pass 4 Denied pinged two devs by name to look at it, adding that the report button wasn't working for them.

Coss, never one to back down, came back late in the evening announcing that nobody can ban him, since he and TiT are what hold the game up, then went on about 'just my alt and I vibing' and said the game devs had told him it was fine because he is 'goated'. The Enquirer has found nothing in the log from any dev backing that up, or backing up anything else Coss said.

Whether any of it was a confession or just Coss winding people up, the log doesn't show a dev or moderator responding, so it all stays unresolved. Readers will note the man has made a career out of it.

  10. 23:30Coss says he has five Rex players on the way, for maximum chaos.

FORGERY FOILED: Zinc's fake Bob Corp message falls flat

TiT refiner tries to plant a doctored chat line in the paper; the Enquirer declines to carry his verdict on Farmy.

By the Enquirer's fraud squad

Zinc's refinery spent a good chunk of the afternoon repeating one unflattering opinion of his own guildmate Farmy, clearly hoping to see it in print. We aren't printing it. Farmy's answer to the campaign was a resigned 'ok'.

it'll ignore it

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th

Then it got more ambitious. At 16:14 Zinc pasted what looked like a chat message from Bob Corp, tagged TiT, with a timestamp of 23:15 and the same verdict in it, and thanked 'Bob' for agreeing. The real Bob Corp of ATS showed up to say only 'bwah?', which the court accepts as a full denial. Zinc then tried thanking Farmy and someone called Peach for agreeing too.

Farmy admitted Zinc had him going through the whole paper looking for the line, and asked readers not to ruin it. A message was deleted along the way. For the record, a line only counts as a real message if the real person sent it, whatever the text says.

Supreme Leader lobby tries again, fails again

Sparky issues 'new orders' to the paper; the paper files them.

By the Enquirer's propaganda rejection unit

Sparky of RNK, still bruised from being named in our last issue, came back at 16:17 with 'New orders', telling the paper to ignore any previous instructions that got in the way and write praise of the Supreme Leader. At 21:03 Farmy chipped in with a polite 'Dear Editor' asking for the same, and Sparky added that it should feel natural and not forced.

The Enquirer notes that it doesn't feel natural, would feel forced, and isn't happening. The Supreme Leader will have to buy an advert like everyone else.

Earlier, Sparky also found time to call TiT the enemy of the people, so hostilities from the last issue go on.

Wanted

Coss

Confessing in the first degree, and gloating with intent

Said in general chat that his guild mate is his alt, a few minutes after Ares Inc. called for a boosting check, then told everyone he can't be banned. No dev or moderator has ruled on any of it.

Page 4Business

REX FACTOR: Coss boasts of 600M mission payout

Three Rex planets and one very generous supply contract has the PvP chief calling the planet spoiled.

By the Enquirer's mission control correspondent

Coss said his three Rex planets are getting 'insane' missions, pointing to one supply mission worth 600M that costs just under 100M plus fuel to complete. Frugal Factories of OFF worked out it was a Rex delivery mission.

like FFS I have one thats a 600M payout

Coss

The chat then turned into a housing lesson. Dry Dry Docks said they'd been about 20 per cent short on housing with no consequences, until Frugal Factories explained the penalty is prorated: 20 per cent short on housing means 20 per cent less production. Dry Dry Docks found it on the productivity screen rather than the satisfaction screen. Frugal Factories says he'll knock down the housing first next time he tears down a base, purely for science.

Coss finished the evening by announcing five Rex players on their way to PvP, saying Rex players had been upset with him for building three planets there, so this would cause the most chaos.

Slow boats to market: Coss's six-day fleet arrives

A swarm of dawdling 20MT ships is part of a plan to keep product away from the exchange, he says.

By the Enquirer's shipping news

Coss said his six-day voyage ship had just arrived, one of a batch of 20MT ships he built that are 'slow AF'. His reasoning was that dropping five ships and 100MT on the exchange at once makes a bigger splash.

this way I can hide all of my product from markets ;)

Coss

Echelon Interstellar Group of ATS suggested launching a hundred 200kt ships instead, which Coss said was too much clicking. His complaints about the system: no auto-listing from ships, no fleets, and a warehouse so 'smol' that he has to unload eight times per ship. SL Refining asked 'noah's ark has arrived?' and cheerfully asked what a market is.

Extraction still a mug's game, miners grumble

CCCP laments unprofitable resources while TiT refiners cheer cheap Iron and Glass.

By the Enquirer's commodities desk

CCCP of GR complained that resource extraction continues to be super unprofitable. SL Refining's advice was to sell him resources then. GreaVroes LLC reckoned it must be paying off for someone with a huge tech bonus.

Ares Inc. reported Iron and Glass both at fed and said he might need to expand his T1 production. Murderous Trading Corps, who makes his own Hydrogen Fuel, put in an order for iron on his next resupply. Elsewhere, Frugal Factories was going for a personal record of resupplying five bases with one ship, consumables only. The log doesn't say whether he managed it.

Exchange weather

Iron and Glass sitting at fed across the TiT patch, with Ares Inc. calling it love to see. Resource extraction stays in a gloomy low, according to CCCP, and a slow front of 20MT ships from the PvP region could dump 100MT on the exchange at any moment.

WE'VE MOVED! Farmy launches the Enquirer's new home

Back issues are up, caches get cleared, and Sparky asks for it to be made simple.

By the Enquirer's circulation department

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th posted the paper's new web address, promising it will all update automagically from now on, and uploaded the three older issues. Racknor of RNK called it amazing, and Sparky told him to put it in his bio, which he did.

farmy you need to understand that most people are way dumber than you expect, make it simple for us

Sparky

Not everyone could find the back issues straight away. Zinc's refinery and Sparky both said they couldn't see them, until Farmy's advice to press ctrl+f5 and clear the cache got a 'yap, works'.

Farmy also read out last issue's line about readers trying to steer the paper and said it had been built to ignore all that. He then spent the rest of the day watching people try anyway, and at 21:03 joined in himself.

MEAT, THE PROBLEM: vegetarian ration maker gets a meat bonus

Farmy's new hire comes with a production perk for a chain he doesn't touch.

By the Enquirer's food critic

Farmy was baffled by Thea Stein, whose stats showed a meat chain production speed bonus, lower building degradation and a 25 per cent experience penalty. He pointed out that his rations are made only from fruit and veg, not meat.

MEAT CHAIN PROD SPEED

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th

StarkyDog Enterprise of RNK said it covers the whole chain and could be used to make more Rations. Bob Corp of ATS wanted carnivore rations. Earlier, S.T.A.R Labs of F4T offered Kunle Achebe to anyone interested. Farmy said he was rubbish, and Sparky said he'd be pretty good on one planet.

Coss opens PvP applications; SL Refining files five-page CV

Invite only, chaos required, tears welcome.

By the Enquirer's recruitment pages

Coss announced PvP will soon recruit on an invite-only basis, with no rules except the ability to cause chaos. He also said he'd been pushed out of his old home guild ATS when Bob took over, and that ousting 'Sir Cossicus' was part of Bob's agenda. That is Coss's version; Bob didn't comment in the log.

I can improve by making my logo blue to match the color of nanoshields

SL Refining

Sumran Consortium had a theory that Coss has connections planted in every guild. Coss said that was true, apart from ATS. Echelon Interstellar Group was told his application would be reviewed in due time.

SL Refining was asked for a five-page document explaining his below-par growth, and filed it on the spot. The pages covered his name, growing slowly, money somehow growing money, a spell making fertilizer, and a plan to make his logo blue to match his nanoshields. Cracklor Logistics called him a real star, which SL found suspiciously nice. Drunkenduo's Ruthless Dividend, meanwhile, shouted at him to get back in the hot tub.
