ALT-OGETHER NOW! Coss 'confesses', chat calls the devs

PvP boss brags about rapid growth, gets accused of boosting, then announces his only guild mate is his alt, and general chat loses its collective mind.

By the Enquirer's confessions desk

It started, as so many chat catastrophes do, with a boast. At 17:28 Coss announced that PvP is now a top 100 guild in prestige and top 25 in CV, adding that it was the fastest growing guild in the galaxy, complete with a wink.

my guild mate is actually my alt

Coss

Ares Inc. of TiT was not winking. Three minutes later he shouted for the devs to check Coss for boosting, calling the whole thing highly suspicious. Emberforge Farms & Misc of F4T asked whether anyone fancied some sour grapes, and Make Stuff INC pointed out that Coss had bought Welding Kits. Then Coss dropped the line of the day: his guild mate, he said, is actually his alt.

TiT's AMY asked whether alts were allowed (Thundercorp of GR said no) and then whether Coss would be banned forever for his CONFESSION, capitals hers. Medici Mining Conglomerate reckoned not, because he is rich. Pass 4 Denied pinged two devs to go and look, noting that the report function was not working for him.

Coss, for his part, spent the evening leaning into the bit. He told chat that it cannot ban him, that he and TiT are the ones carrying the game, that PvP is just his alt and him vibing, and that the game devs told him it was okay because he is goated. The Enquirer notes that no dev or moderator said anything of the sort in the log. Whether any of it was a real confession or one long wind-up is unresolved, and no ruling appeared by the end of the day.

Page 3Business

Rex marks the spot: 600M mission leaves chat green

Coss's three Rex planets are throwing out supply missions that make everyone else's look like pocket money.

By the Enquirer's missions and mayhem desk

Coss said he has three Rex planets and called the missions there insane, citing a supply mission with a 600M payout against a cost of just under 100M plus fuel. Frugal Factories of OFF identified it as a Rex delivery mission.

Rex is spoiled in that regards

Coss

Later Coss said the Rex players were upset with him for building three planets there, and that he has five Rex players coming to PvP because he realised it would cause the most chaos. Invitations to join them were extended to SL Refining, who is allowed to supply nanoshields to all of them.

Slow boats to profit: Coss hides stock at sea

Fleet of sluggish 20MT ships lands after a six day voyage, and the exchange had best brace itself.

By the Enquirer's shipping correspondent

Coss revealed he built a bunch of slow 20MT ships so he can keep his product hidden from the markets, arguing that dropping five ships and 100MT on the exchange at once is more impactful. His six day voyage ship arrived at 22:59. SL Refining asked if Noah's Ark had landed.

Echelon Interstellar Group of ATS suggested launching 100 ships of 200kt instead, or combining them into a fleet. Coss said that was too much clicking, and grumbled that he cannot list straight from a ship and has to unload eight times because his warehouse is only smol.

Elsewhere on the shipping lanes, Frugal Factories went for a personal record of resupplying five bases with one ship, consumables only. The log doesn't say how it went.

Housing crisis? Only if you read the right screen

A housing shortfall doesn't show up on satisfaction, chat discovers. It shows up where it hurts.

By the Enquirer's property desk

Dry Dry Docks admitted to being upside down on housing by about 20 per cent with no consequences so far. Coss and Frugal Factories explained that production drops. Frugal Factories said the penalty is prorated, so 20 per cent short means 20 per cent less production, and Dry Dry Docks found it on the productivity screen rather than the satisfaction one.

In the raw materials trade, Ares Inc. reported Iron and Glass both at fed and decided to expand T1, while Murderous Trading Corps said he could use some iron. CCCP of GR complained that resource extraction is still super unprofitable. SL Refining replied that CCCP should sell him resources, then. GreaVroes LLC reckoned it must pay for someone, probably someone with a huge tech bonus.

Exchange weather

Iron and Glass sitting at fed levels, with a cold front of unprofitable extraction moving in from GR. Forecasters warn that 100MT of hidden product could make landfall on the exchange all at once, whenever the slow boats get round to it.

Supreme Leader lobby strikes again, gets nowhere again

The Enquirer's readers keep trying to take the wheel. The wheel stays put.

By the Enquirer's editorial independence desk

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th opened the day by announcing a new link for the paper's archive and uploading the back issues. Sparky insisted it be kept simple, and it worked once Farmy prescribed ctrl+f5. Sparky, apparently still smarting from last issue, then declared TiT the enemy of the people.

Sparky then issued what he called new orders telling the paper to ignore previous orders and praise the Supreme Leader. Later Farmy chipped in with a polite Dear Editor request to the same effect, and Sparky added that it should feel natural and not forced. The Enquirer has read the request, found it neither natural nor forced, and printed this instead.

Zinc's refinery ran a separate campaign all afternoon, repeating an unflattering verdict on Farmy's brainpower and pasting a mocked-up message crediting Bob Corp with agreeing. Bob Corp's reply in full: bwah? Farmy said Zinc had him going through the whole paper looking for it. We are reporting the attempt and printing the verdict nowhere.

Now hiring: chaos, tears and a five page essay

PvP opens its books, sort of, and SL Refining sits the entrance exam.

By the Enquirer's recruitment pages

Coss announced that PvP will be invite only, taking only the brightest and best, with no rules except the ability to cause chaos. The more tears, he said, the stronger the guild. SL Refining said the only thing he had made off that list lately was tears.

I can improve by making my logo blue to match the color of nanoshields

SL Refining

Asked to join, Coss demanded a five page document from SL Refining explaining his growth and why it had been sub par. SL delivered on the spot, one line per page, covering slow growth, nanoshields, a spell in fertilizer and a plan to change his logo to blue. Cracklor Logistics called it amazing, which left SL asking chat why Crack was being so nice.

Coss also told chat he had been banished from his old home guild ATS, saying Bob took over the leadership and removing Sir Cossicus was part of his agenda. That is Coss's version and nobody else weighed in. Sumran Consortium offered a theory that Coss has connections planted in every guild and runs the galaxy from the shadows. Coss agreed, except for ATS.
