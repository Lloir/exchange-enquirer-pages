DRONE ALONE! Coss says 'my alt', then claims half the galaxy

A mystery PvP member pushes Consumer Electronics, gets asked who he is, and chat falls down an alt-shaped hole it may never climb out of.

By the Enquirer's identity crisis desk

It started with a bit of career advice. When Pear Inc. wondered aloud about switching to science, a PvP company called Drone inc. popped up to suggest going all-in on Consumer Electronics instead, citing the power of knock-off brand recognition. Earlier that evening Pear had already told chat he was watching Coss talk to his alt in guild chat, calling it quite entertaining.

Vent it towards me, I'm happy being the GT villain now

Coss

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th asked Drone inc. who they were. Coss answered in two words: "my alt". Farmy promptly posted a link to the one-account policy in the game rules, and AMY tagged two developers to ask whether the rules on alts would actually be enforced.

Then things got silly. Coss announced he also had 4 alts in TiT and 6 in ATS, and started naming them: Cracklor, Ares, Amy and Drunk, plus Echelon, Lek, Tato and Twisted. Medici Mining Conglomerate volunteered as the GP alt, explaining that everyone talks to themselves because general chat is too empty. Mittcb, reviving the theme of our last issue, simply declared "I'm Sparticus". Twice.

Entropy raised the obvious point: maybe none of them are alts at all. Makuria Inc thought Coss wasn't hurting anyone and should be left to chat with himself in peace. By morning Drone inc. had described itself as a willess vessel for the ambitions of the GOAT, and Farmy had settled on a verdict of his own: it's Coss all the way down.

No developer response appears in the log. Whether Drone inc. is a real alt, a very committed joke, or a fellow with a strong opinion on consumer electronics is, for now, unresolved.

#FreeRedshift: TiT boss pleads for jailed member

If alts are running wild, asks Farmy, why is Redshift still behind bars?

By our man outside the prison gates

Farmy McFarm Face The 7th tagged a developer to ask for the release of TiT's Redshift Endeavors from jail, on the grounds that there's bigger offending going on lately. Asked what he meant, he said some people are saying they have alts. The log doesn't say what Redshift was jailed for, and Hydrogenica Energies' question on that point went unanswered.

#BAN FARMY MCFARM FACE THE 7TH, BRING ON THE 8TH!

Voidsteel Conglomerate

Ares Inc. launched #FreeRedshift. Makuria Inc answered with #BanTiT, which Ares described as very hurtful, before being offered some Flux on the house as compensation. Makuria also called the whole thing a bit of an altercation, which this paper wishes it had thought of first.

Voidsteel Conglomerate then proposed banning Farmy and bringing on the 8th. Farmy, for reasons best known to himself, reposted it word for word before asking "wait what?" Make Stuff INC seconded the motion shortly after. Redshift's release, like the 8th Farmy, remains pending.

No lowballers! Research fire sale ends in lowballing

Pass 4 Denied names his terms, the market names its price, and the Quantum Research Data just keeps going round.

By the Enquirer's science desk

Pass 4 Denied came to chat needing to restock like a madman and selling Quantum Research Data, with one condition: no lowballers. Zinc's refinery offered 32k. Make Stuff INC offered to do one better at 31k. Farmy McFarm Face The 7th then announced he had lowballed and won, apparently by claiming it was for his kids' Christmas present.

No lowballers, I know what I've got

Pass 4 Denied

Not long after, Mittcb was back in chat trying to resell the research, saying he'd bought all of Pass 4 Denied's lot and then realised he didn't have any money to invest. Frugal Factories reported that Lek had already undercut him. Farmy declared that the trade wars had begun.

Lek then gave the gloomy forecast: the science market is getting crowded and everyone will suffer unless some producers switch away soon. He said it hadn't helped that Coss and Aquaman pushed almost 3M units over the last few weeks. Earlier, Ares Inc. had recalled happily buying at 45 to 50k before the crash.

There is at least one big buyer about. Pear Inc. says he needs to push 6 research levels in 2 weeks, about 145k quantum. Over in RNK, Sparky explained that a single level now costs 2500 quantum while he makes 700 a day, which puts his polymath dreams roughly 40 days away if everyone else stops.

Tato turns chem into a grudge match

ATS's chem champion promises to wreck a rival's flips, even if it costs her.

By our chemical warfare correspondent

Late-night chat saw Tato Industries lay out an unusual business plan. With Coss respeccing into chem for a few weeks, Tato said there were no hard feelings but that she had made a game of making sure he doesn't make money off chem. Manufacturing profits are fine, she said, but no flipping for profit. Coss replied: challenge accepted.

no, i'd rather make it difficult for you, even if i suffer

Tato Industries

Tato admitted the pressure means she now keeps stockpiles to defend her realm, something she never used to do, and that downturns in chem have turned her into a filthy flipper too. She also said she's racing Twilight for the top spot, and that getting herself and ATS to number one is what keeps her playing.

It led into a long debate about prestige. Hydrogenica Energies said the prestige nerf broke ATS's momentum at a time when they were ahead on CV and close on prestige. Lek called mission rerolling insanely strong. Medici Mining Conglomerate said he doesn't reroll missions at all, being stingy that way, and later agreed that for ROI it's the market these days.

Old-timers' hour: the tools that built the galaxy

Trackers, spreadsheets and the site nearly everyone admits they use.

By the Enquirer's heritage desk

Just after midnight on the 27th, chat went misty-eyed. Sumran Consortium remembered an early activity tracker that mapped when companies were online. Ares Inc. described TiT's old guild website for tracking contracts. Sumran also recalled smuggling Quantum Emitters through the exchange because contracts didn't exist yet, and Medici recalled saving up two to three days of inputs for a round trip to the rim.

idk, monthly sub sounds so scummy

Lek

Lek pointed out that when someone is online is public knowledge, but said even some ATS members were against making it easy to see. Medici then shared some figures: only 50% of players under $1B CV use gtc, and 8 to 24% of those between $1B and $100B don't. "How do these guys survive?" he asked.

Chat suggested Lek should charge a subscription. Lek refused. He did announce a new gt-c update, with mission perks now valued and a much nicer executive solver, and said he's toying with ideas for a project of his own, with no details yet. Makuria Inc reckoned nobody of appreciable size would be here without it.

Exchange weather

Aluminium Ore warming to what Farmy called a little expensive after GP's Awaitafar raised prices, though traders don't trust the front. Scattered Fruits green at $42.50, heavy Quantum Research Data showers over the science district, and a big polyethylene front rolling in from PvP by morning.

Page 5Society & culture

Have couch, will travel: Pear's guild crawl continues

F4T, then PvP, then who knows. The galaxy's most dedicated sofa-surfer is looking for his next host.

By the Enquirer's travel desk

Pear Inc. is on tour. By his own account he joined F4T one day and PvP the next morning, and he's been asking chat for any guild happy to host a hobo pear, promising not to leak on the couch (much). SL Refining suggested pouring beer on it to cover things up.

its a guild crawl

Pear Inc.

He even pitched KOI in Chinese. Roughly translated, the message said he was on a hobo guild tour, would only stay a few days, would sit on their sofa, eat their food and meet new friends before moving on, and promised not to cause too much trouble. Farmy replied with a Chinese line that roughly means lost deep in the clouds, whereabouts unknown, and suggested China chat instead.

S.T.A.R Labs of F4T waved him off: "Bye Pear, lovely having you here". By morning he was thanking PvP's Potentia - Vis - Proventus for the couch and the chat, and asking who'd give him a sofa for the night. Zinc's refinery suggested TiT. Entropy, meanwhile, wanted to know if this was round robin or musical chairs.

Cracklor's gap year: out of TiT, into SRS, and back again?

One guild exit, one very short stay, and a door that TiT's GM says is firmly shut.

By the Enquirer's migration desk

Cracklor Logistics. announced he had been deposed and was now adrift, noting that just leaving TiT had cut his consumables to 19 hours. After tagging half of Serious Business to let him in, he was taken in by SRS, and Makuria Inc later thanked him for stopping by.

its cold out here alone

Cracklor Logistics.

The same morning, SRS pulled into number 4 on the leaderboard. Makuria told GR to imagine not being number 4. When Cracklor tried to come home, Farmy McFarm Face The 7th briefly removed every TiT officer and said no. By the next day the officers had quietly been reinstated.

Holue's Galatic Contruction later asked if Cracklor had boosted SRS's prestige during his stay. Cracklor said he hadn't and had only stopped by to say hello. Then he said he'd never been in SRS at all, and wasn't Cracklor either. The paper notes all three claims without choosing one. Makuria, for his part, crowned the pair GT's favourite couple.

Hold the front page! The galaxy's newspaper war

Chat reviews the rival press, and one reader tries a crafty way into print.

By a thoroughly neutral media correspondent

Holue's Galatic Contruction warned readers that the GT weekly, his own hand-made paper, may become bi-weekly unless motivation strikes. That set chat off comparing it with this very publication. Holue wondered, fairly, whether some people just like the Enquirer because they know they'll be in it.

They are both entertaining just different flavors. Farmys feels more like business section and holos feels more like personal stories

Entropy

Entropy gave the most balanced review, saying he found both entertaining and in fact preferred Holue's, praising its hand-made focus and saying it read like someone telling you a story and wandering off down bunny trails. We accept this graciously and with only minor sulking.

A TiT reader also tried to tag this paper to get a message featured, and explained a theory that flooding chat is the way onto the front page. For the record, it isn't. As a reward, the message in question has been left out.
