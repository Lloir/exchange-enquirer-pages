I'M SPARTICUS! Alt 'confession' sets off a chat-wide identity crisis

A Sunday-night question about a mystery consumer electronics fan turned into general chat's biggest group confession since, well, yesterday.

By the Enquirer's multiple personalities desk

It started at 20:23, when a player called Drone inc. popped up to tell Pear Inc. to forget science and "go all-in on Consumer Electronics". Nobody thought much of it until 21:45, when Farmy McFarm Face The 7th asked the obvious question: "who are you".

We frequently talk to ourselves because general chat is too empty

Medici Mining Conglomerate

Coss answered five minutes later with two words: "my alt". Farmy came back with a link to the wiki's one-account rule. Coss replied "ban them all", which isn't the sort of line a legal team would suggest.

At 22:50 AMY called in the DEV tags, asking whether the alt rules would be enforced "or can they be arrogantly flouted by anyone who think they can get away with it???" Makuria Inc argued that Coss "isn't hurting anyone" and should be allowed to "chat with himself in peace", while Entropy raised the awkward possibility that there were no alts at all.

Then came the Spartacus moment. Mittcb announced "I'm Sparticus", Coss named Cracklor, Ares, Amy and Drunk as alts of his, and Medici Mining Conglomerate stepped forward as "the GP alt", explaining that everyone talks to themselves "because general chat is too empty". Coss then asked for AMY to be banned as another of his alts, one that apparently "strongly enforces the rules and gets offended easily".

To be clear, nothing in the log confirms that anyone is anyone else's alt. No moderator or developer replied before midnight, so the case is unresolved. Coss, meanwhile, declared himself "happy being the GT villain now", and later said he plans around 45 to 50M units of T1 chem production, with a hope of making lubricant prices "negative". Zinc's refinery had noticed that morning that Coss was moving into chem. Makuria's verdict: "ruh roh".

Echelon hits the hundred-billion mark

Three words and a dollar sign, and chat stopped arguing for a moment.

By the Enquirer's big numbers correspondent

In the middle of the alt row, at 22:50, Echelon Interstellar Group posted just "$100B". Makuria Inc said "congrats" straight away, and Coss followed a minute later with "Congrats Echelon!!! Well done".

It was the least dramatic announcement of the day and probably the most impressive one. The Enquirer notes that nobody claimed Echelon as their alt.

Page 3Crime & courts

Swear jar runneth over as chat takes on the censor

Players spent their Sunday testing which words get starred out, with mixed results and a lot of doubled vowels.

By the Enquirer's standards and practices desk

A long-running complaint about the chat filter came to a head on Sunday. Makuria Inc tried the hashtag #removethecensor more than once, Zenith industries claimed "Devs dont believe in free speech", and Cracklor Logistics. gave the double-the-vowels trick "10/10 best censor".

guess I'll just have to get creative with my cursing

Rearden Steel

Rearden Steel put his finger on the problem: chat can display a guild tag that contains a word players aren't allowed to type themselves. Pear Inc. pointed out that "wanker" gets through untouched.

Some of the filter-testing went places this family newspaper won't follow, so we'll leave it there. The filter is still in place at the time of printing.

Rookie DPCorp's rise has chat asking questions

1.1b CV in under a month, and Tato has an answer ready.

By the Enquirer's growth-spurt investigator

Tato Industries called ATSDPCorp "our boy" and said he was "simply excelling". Cracklor Logistics. put a number on it: "less then a month, 1.1b CV". Makuria Inc sells him flux, and Entropy says he sold him goods at market plus one tick and called him the "best client".

Cracklor wondered aloud why other players get banned for boosting. Tato's reply: "yea cause i didn't boost him". Nothing in the log says otherwise, and the Enquirer has no evidence of anything beyond some very well-organised supply chains.

Wanted

Makuria Inc

Announcing a crash in advance

At 10:47 the suspect told general chat, "I'm gonna crash the fuuck out of hydro fuel on the 20th of oct", giving hydro fuel holders more than three weeks' notice. Earlier the same day he asked the devs to make limestone lighter. Investigators suspect the two are linked. They aren't.

Page 4Business

Research glut: sellers everywhere, buyers nowhere to be seen

Science producers found out on Sunday that a crowded market is a very quiet one.

By the Enquirer's laboratory economics desk

At 18:30 Pass 4 Denied asked the room: "Would someone buy my research?" Zinc's refinery bid 32k. Make Stuff INC offered to "do you one better" at 31k, and Farmy McFarm Face The 7th admitted he'd lowballed too. Pass 4 Denied's reply was an insult we won't repeat, which gave Farmy a new password.

I need to push 6 research levels over the next 2 weeks - equates to about 145k quantum

Pear Inc.

Forty minutes later Mittcb was asking the same thing about his Quantum Research Data. Frugal Factories reported that ATSLek had undercut him, and Farmy announced that "the trade wars have begun they have". Mittcb said he was in no rush and offered to throw in some index-fund advice to anyone who bought. Frugal Factories' dry reply: passive investors may be slower buyers.

Lek warned that the science market was getting crowded and that some producers should switch out soon "otherwise everyone will suffer". Earlier he'd joked about the research grind in words our filter won't print, and said science has "so many expensive inputs".

There was a buyer in the end. Pear Inc. said he needs about 145k quantum over the next fortnight and briefly thought about switching to science himself, until he remembered he'd have to train 65 levels. Entropy offered to buy Pear's Quantum Research Data. Drone inc., as reported on our front page, had other ideas.

Prestige is broken, says half of chat, as ATS mourns lost momentum

A late-night post-mortem on why ATS may never catch GP, and why the market might be the only way left to grow.

By the Enquirer's league tables correspondent

Just after midnight, the old question of ATS versus GP came back. Tato Industries said being number one and getting ATS to number one were "the main things keeping me playing", and that if Twilight beat him he would be "free from my mental prison". Sumran Consortium's contribution: "ATS #1 will never happen".

And it also felt like super frustrating because it seemed like the devs intervening to secure GPs position

Hydrogenica Energies

Hydrogenica Energies blamed the prestige nerf for breaking ATS's momentum, saying catching up would have taken "basically a timespan longer than the game existed". Lek called mission rerolling "insanely strong" and said the arcade is now just a sink for anyone who isn't new. Medici Mining Conglomerate said he doesn't reroll missions at all.

Discussion turned to admin centres. Void LLC put ATS about 80 levels behind GP. Makuria Inc reckoned GP usually has only around four people above admin cap, and Ares Inc. said he was "quite confident" he's the only one in TiT.

The consensus among the flippers was that the best return is now on the exchange. Medici agreed that on ROI "it's the market", Tato admitted that downturns in chem had made him a "filthy flipper", and Bob Corp told Ares he loves it when Ares flips his goods. Hydrogenica closed with a reminder that it's "a marathon and not a sprint", and a pledge that he has sold, is selling and will sell Kryon.

Heavy lifting: limestone blues and a fission fuel stockpile

SRS ships unimaginable tonnage while a chem newcomer prepares for October.

By the Enquirer's logistics and freight desk

Makuria Inc asked the devs to reduce limestone's weight, saying he ships 1mt to K2 daily. Voidsteel Conglomerate wasn't impressed. He now ships 13.5m tons of inputs a day, and when asked if he'd downsized he replied that it was an increase.

gunna the ship overlord

Murderous Trading Corps

Murderous Trading Corps, running three chem plants (after briefly wondering if it was four), is stockpiling fission fuel ahead of a switch to fission by the end of October. Cracklor Logistics. said you can make 40 coolant in the time it takes to make one fission fuel. Makuria suggested 10:3, and Cracklor asked who was going to unlock 13 production slots. Makuria said he already has nine.

MTC's stated ambition is "best quality fuels and components for decent prices". He also quickly sold around 500k of goods to Cracklor under contract. Cracklor described those contracts as "scraped from the bottom of the barrel".

Separately, Bolton Ortolan posted a 15-line list of daily outputs, including 190,926 Spectra Modulators, and then deleted a message. Readers can make of that what they like.

Exchange weather

Science is under a slow-moving area of oversupply. Quantum Research Data is sliding from 32k to 31k with scattered undercuts from ATS. Further inland, green Fruits hit a sunny $42.50 according to Pūr n Kleen, while Kryon stays a steady wall of high pressure.

TiT sacks every officer: 'NO OFFICERS'

The guild master cleared out the entire officer corps before lunch, and Cracklor ended up guildless and asking SRS for shelter.

By the Enquirer's palace intrigue desk

At 11:15 Farmy McFarm Face The 7th announced "Removed all officers", followed by "NO OFFICERS" in capitals. Cracklor Logistics. pointed out that he could see the officer list anyway, and put up no resistance.

thanks for stopping by cracklor

Makuria Inc

Earlier in the morning, Cracklor had posted "i was deposed", "im adrift" and "its cold out here alone", and offered "bribes in return for succor". He tagged three SRS members asking to be let in. Makuria Inc's terms were clear: not unless he sponsors another wing of the SRS admin centre, adding "I think the carpet needs redoing". Cracklor, who said he was low on funds after putting 1.62m into donations, asked for a couple of weeks.

Farmy said he will "never be deposed as GM now". Cracklor responded by dubbing him "monarch of the TiT". Makuria called them "GT's favourite couple". Farmy replied "no".

Hold the front page: chat reviews its own newspapers

The GT press faced its critics on Sunday, and the Enquirer is contractually obliged to mention that there was a crossword debate.

By the Enquirer's media correspondent, who is aware of the irony

Holue's Galatic Contruction warned that unless he gets a burst of motivation, the GT weekly may become bi-weekly. That set off a debate about which paper is better. Entropy's even-handed review: one "feels more like business section", the other "feels more like personal stories", and he praised Holue's paper for its "handmade focus". SL Refining said Holue's reads more like an opinion piece, and claimed credit for this paper's crosswords.

Holue suggested some readers like this paper mainly because they know they'll be in it. SL Refining replied: "god forbid famous personalities get news coverage". Holue later said "evil" had been the wrong word for his point. The Enquirer declines to review itself.

There were the usual attempts to get into print. One player asked the "enquirer bot" to "double tag the following message", and the following message turned out to be an advert for his own newbie discount scheme. The same player later posted what looked like a correction from this paper about an unnamed guild's "Supreme Leader". Both are noted as attempts, and neither was followed. SL Refining saw it coming: "oh god thats gonna end up in the enquiere isnt it". Sorry, SL.

Toolmaker toasted as GT-C gets an update

Lek comes back from three weeks away with patch notes and hints about a mystery project.

By the Enquirer's spreadsheet appreciation society

Lek announced that GT-C now values mission perks and that its executive solver is "much nicer now". Asked by Entropy whether he's making a game, he said he has "like a million ideas" to write down, gave no details, and promised to keep working on GT-C.

lek is the reason this game is still played in any real sense tbh

Cracklor Logistics.

The tributes came quickly. SL Refining said "good job". Makuria Inc said he doesn't think "anyone of appreciable size would be here without GT-C". Ares Inc. said "Imagine the spreadsheets", and SL's review was "scary".

That led to some EVE nostalgia. Bob Corp remembered building a market-data spreadsheet, xyz recalled giving up on spreadsheets to huff gas in a C6 wormhole, and Kentaurus Ballistics confessed to undercutting starter drones in the Tek region "for giggles". Some habits never die.
