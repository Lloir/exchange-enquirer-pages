Pulse of the Galaxy

Gossipy, sleepy and waiting on an update

The galaxy spent Wednesday playing guild musical chairs and loving every minute. Coss's one-day tour of PvP, GP and TiT pulled in half the server as commentators, Pear was saying goodbyes before breakfast and talking about the TiT campfire by teatime, and a run of 'when is Coss' puns kept chat busy all morning. Nobody seemed short of things to say about who belongs where.

Under the gossip there's a restless edge. The auction house got another eye-roll over its timeframe, Skye Blu Foods moaned about waiting for 'the next 2.5% passive bonus', Berlin food distributions said the game was 'getting a little stale', and an unusual number of players were comparing their oldest execs and dreading retirement bills. Fun crowd, slightly bored game. Chat also went well off the rails late at night in a direction this paper won't be following.

HOMELESS NO MORE

COSS-MIC JOURNEY: KICKED, SNUBBED AND BACK IN TiT BY BEDTIME

In a single Wednesday the galaxy's best-known tycoon was booted by his own drone, left waiting on a tied vote at GP, and ended up wearing a TiT tag again.

By the Enquirer's transfer-window desk

It started, as these things apparently now do, with a letter to this newspaper. At 07:07 Drone inc., signing off as 'Aspiring Guild Leader', complained that 'your little rag' had misrepresented the cookies it bakes. It insisted they are crunchy outside, chewy inside and baked only for PvP members. The Enquirer stands by its crumbs. Chat quickly decided Drone was Toys back in a new chassis (Farmy McFarm Face The 7th: 'as a drone yes'), and Drone spent the morning hinting it would take PvP off Coss, while Coss played along as the obedient 'alt'.

Drone kicked me, I’ve hit rock bottom

Coss

By lunchtime the joke had turned real. aldera mining company told chat that Coss wanted to join GP and the vote to let him in was tied, then asked whether lobbying was legal, offering to sell 'some very expensive Copper Wire' at double the price if the devs approved. Farmy advised GP against it, saying Coss was 'looking for intel', and Drone reacted with mock horror at the 'Double-crossing'. Bribery has never been so openly priced in wire.

The vote never went his way. At 18:06 aldera announced 'i was in fact, not bribed', Farmy declared Coss homeless, and Coss confirmed he'd been booted from PvP by the drone he supposedly trained. Drone said he 'failed to adhere to the requirements' and then, being Drone, posted a job ad for any '100B+ chads' to take his place.

Help came from the old firm. Pear Inc. said TiT had extended an invitation after 'a long internal chat', calling Coss 'a force' who 'has given a lot to the game and its lore'. That's a generous send-off from a guild he left, and by 23:00 Holue's Galatic Contruction was addressing 'TiTCoss' and asking what happened to the PvP gimmick. Coss said the devs had 'scolded' him once they learnt the plan again, promised '30 planets snd 2000% overhead in 48 hours', and predicted TiT would outgrow ATS 'On a % base'. The Enquirer notes that small caveat and is saving this page for Friday.

PEAR LEAVES AGAIN: THE FRUIT THAT KEEPS ON ROLLING

Our previous issue's cover star said goodbye in the morning and was missing the TiT campfire by evening.

By the Enquirer's orchard correspondent

Asked by Farmy at 09:32 whether they were coming back, Pear Inc. said they were 'Just about to say my goodbyes'. Sumran Consortium promised a fruit salad for the leaving do, which the Enquirer finds tactless and also delicious, and Kraken Org called pears 'the candy of nature'.

Where the fruit landed was barely a mystery. Pear offered a TiT invite to the first person to send a chicken (Farmy: 'no you won't'), YoYo Ltd. asked if it was too late to welcome Pear, and by the evening Pear was telling Drone to 'Come join tit' and saying they'd missed 'sitting around the TiT campfire'. Cracklor Logistics. has already named it, listing 'the drd era, the pear era, now the tit era'. Readers who took last week's headline literally will want to update their spreadsheets.

Page 3Business

ARES INC. BREAKS 100B, GETS PAID IN CHEESE

A three-digit milestone, a pile of congratulations and, briefly, a guild trying to buy him with chickens.

By the Enquirer's big numbers desk

At 11:33 Ares Inc. announced '100B' with three party poppers, and chat obliged. Drone inc. offered camembert, Voidsteel Conglomerate and Drowne said congrats, and Makuria Inc. added theirs later. Camembert is not legal tender, but the Enquirer accepts it for subscriptions.

Ares spent part of the afternoon offering to bribe GP to take him instead of Coss, payment 'in the form of Chickens'. Cracklor Logistics. ruled that 'ares isnt for sale', and Farmy countered that he is 'for loan'. The market for 100B players apparently runs on leasing.

Elsewhere on the leaderboards, Holue's Galatic Contruction got congratulations on a construction medal and said GR and SRS 'might actually catch TIT in CV pretty soon'. Makuria put SRS 120m behind, Holue corrected that to 220m once 'the Coss trade' registers on GT-C, and Coss replied 'No'. Short, if not exactly a rebuttal.

MONTH-END SPIKE WARNING: GP MEMBERS CRAMMING THEIR DONATIONS

Medici reveals why some goods jump on the last day of the month, and it's a homework problem.

By the Enquirer's warehouse correspondent

Medici Mining Conglomerate explained that GP has a mandatory guild donation percentage, so from the 15th and 'especially the last day of the month' donatable goods spike as members hit their quota at the last minute. Today being the 30th, traders are warned. Holue suggested doing it weekly instead, which is sensible enough that nobody will.

Medici also bragged that GP's warehouse now stands at 10m tons, handy for hiding inputs when prices spike, and said they want it 'at least 300'. Makuria Inc., on 'just shy of 3m', called that nice and explained they mostly buy from inside their guild anyway. Exchange price watchers, take note: the biggest guild is building somewhere to hide its stock.

45 BASES, 6 SHIPS, ONE STARTER PACK LATER

Chat talks a holdout into his first microtransaction ever.

By the Enquirer's consumer affairs desk

aldera mining company asked whether anyone in the top 10 hadn't bought the starter pack, then admitted to playing since March and running 45 bases on six ships without it. That's either heroic or a cry for help, and Bob Corp seemed to think so too, warning of spending 'every moment of your life sending ships'.

The sales pitch came from all sides: Voidsteel Conglomerate, Metanoia, AeriCore Industries ('ships twice as fast? Yes please!') and EggInASac all vouched for it. At 17:23 aldera reported 'i did it', calling it the first time in their life they'd spent money on microtransactions. AeriCore called it a nice tip to the devs. The Enquirer calls it peer pressure, and very effective peer pressure at that.

Exchange weather

Calm through mid-month, then a sharp front of donatable goods moving in from GP on the 30th as members scramble to meet quota. Copper wire may see isolated bribe-related showers, though aldera reports none actually fell.

MAKURIA'S SHIPS LEARN TO FEED THEMSELVES

A homebrew extension auto-repairs and refuels, while SL Refining takes a bow for planet nicknames.

By the Enquirer's gadgets desk

Makuria Inc. spent the morning explaining a quality-of-life extension that tries to repair and refuel ships when the ship popup appears, with contracts possibly next. At 13:28 came the announcement in capitals: the ships now auto refuel and repair. Cracklor Logistics. asked to be signed up, while also admitting it 'means nothing to me but sounds cool'. Makuria promises to release it with everything toggleable, which is a level of thoughtfulness this paper rarely gets to report.

MY SHIPS AUTO REFUEL AND REPAIR TiTCracklor Logistics. I'VE DONE IT

Makuria Inc

SL Refining was proud of contributing planet nicknames to the latest update (right click planet names in the base tab). Holue's Galatic Contruction grumbled that it overwrites the base name, SL said you can add it back, and Cracklor revealed his own contribution was the 'hours until wh is full' readout. Small features, sure, but at least chat is getting updates from somewhere.

EXEC SWAP SHOP: KENTA OUT, ELSIE IN, COLE CROWNED

A busy day in the specialist trade, with retirement fear in the air.

By the Enquirer's human resources desk

SL Refining offered Kenta Watanabe to 'who wants it', wifimike bid 'me', and the deal closed with wifimike handing over Elsie Hargreaves. A clean trade, which by this galaxy's standards is suspicious in itself.

wifimike later celebrated 'FINALLY GOT A GOOD ONE' in Cole Emerson, Skye Blu Foods called it a 'drugless chad', and Cracklor reckoned Cole was close to ideal for a resex base. Meanwhile Isotope Incorporated admitted their oldest exec, at 50, sits unused because she's 'kinda bad', and Makuria wants to offload limestone and mining drill specialists 'before they die of old age'. Planet Nibbler, Zinc's refinery and Entropy all reported no retirements yet, which is exactly what people say right before one happens.

The Enquirer Says

OUR VERDICT: THE AUCTION HOUSE CAN'T COME SOON ENOUGH

Holue's Galatic Contruction reckons resex players will gain most from the auction house, because everyone will dump their non-core specialists. wifimike asked when it's coming, got an answer, and Cracklor Logistics. pointed out that 'thats the timeframe they said last time'. By the afternoon Murderous Trading Corps was asking whether Farmy's chicken races would come before or after 'the auction the debs promised'.

This paper doesn't make release dates, but it does notice a queue. Makuria is holding specialists that are slowly ageing, Isotope has an exec on the bench, and Skye Blu Foods is bored waiting for the next 2.5% bonus. Give these people somewhere to sell their pensioners.

— The Editor
