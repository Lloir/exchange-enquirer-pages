TiT's Chicken-Themed Broads Go Viral

A poster about Long Do and Wanqing becomes the unexpected must-see meme of the week.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

TiT's latest poster about Long Do and Wanqing's questionable 'together ❤️' scenario has become the day's must-see meme. Chickens, suggestive poses, and one ill-advised allusion to a fictional respiratory condition later, the stars align for what could be the most viral moment of the year. But is it really the worst music video ever made, or just oddly endearing in its chaos?

Pear Inc. has already started sketching out revisions to the poster while the internet roars with laughter and horrified fascination, making this possibly the only exec-led meme to take off on GT servers.

Will it outstrip the traffic that the last major TiT scandal pulled in, or is the viral moment a passing trend? Stay tuned as we chart the rise and fall of the music video that launched a thousand gifs.
