Pulse of the Galaxy

Giddy in the tinkering shed, gloomy about the long run

Two moods ran side by side all day. The overnight crowd took a dim view of the galaxy's future: Coss called it a shame the game is dying, Holue's Galatic Contruction reckoned it all hangs on the mobile app (and that if the app flops, the game's dead), Sumran Consortium admitted a newcomer would feel swamped in a galaxy where everyone already knows each other, and Coss predicted GP will soon be 200 admin levels clear of everyone. Nobody argued very hard, which tells you something.

Then the sun came up and the same playerbase spent ten hours gleefully breaking a fan-made script, translating each other into Dutch, Welsh and Wingdings, and racing ships around the galaxy on a website held together with hope. The Enquirer's reading: players are worried about whether the game has a future, and dealing with it the healthy way, by building toys and refusing to go to bed. The loudest shared gripe of the day was not prices but the missing auction house.

DELETED

WING AND A PRAYER: FARMY'S SCRIPT DINGS OUT WINGDINGS

A homemade UI script gave general chat instant translation, and general chat used it to communicate exclusively in tiny pictures until its creator pulled the plug.

By the Enquirer's decoding desk

Farmy Mc Farm Face the 9th's Sunday began, as all the best Sundays do, with a system notice. At 09:21 YoYo Ltd. posted an unbalanced trading pattern warning showing a net loss of 106,538$ on trades with Farmy. Makuria Inc cried boosting, Zinc's refinery guessed a ban, and Farmy laughed it off. Later in the day he explained it: he had reset, and his old trades were still counting against him, including a pre-reset sale that S.T.A.R Labs dug out of their history: 342 Industrial Machinery at 8,000.00$ each on 2 October. No ban, no verdict, just a man who could briefly not buy any milk. This paper files it under paperwork, not scandal.

FARMY FIX THIS I NEED TO WING MY DING IN EVERY LANGUAGE

Makuria Inc

Undeterred, Farmy then released his Tampermonkey script, Galactic Tycoons Sleek UI, and Makuria appointed himself chief tester, which in practice meant finding a new bug roughly every four minutes. The sidebar broke, his money figure 'broke free', the HR tab lost its label, and at one point viewing someone else's profile gave Makuria Farmy's prestige and value. 'WILL YOU STOP BREAKING THINGS,' wailed Farmy. 'I'M JUST REALLY GOOD AT TESTING THINGS,' came the reply. Both, this paper rules, were correct.

The headline feature was a translator running on Google's API, and chat celebrated by speaking every language except English. Drone inc. rolled out a Dutch tongue-twister and a Frisian shibboleth about bread, butter and green cheese, Farmy dipped into Welsh, Irish, Tamil and Russian, and CaraDePercata, apparently suspecting the whole game was AI, asked in Portuguese what everyone had for lunch yesterday, the classic test for robots. Nobody passed or failed.

Then came the Wingdings. What started as a cheeky exchange of secret-club messages between Farmy and Makuria escalated when Zenith industries posted a towering wall of symbols that, by our decoding desk's reckoning, is a lengthy ode to someone called Gaster. Farmy asked him to chill. Zenith promised to stop. Twenty minutes later Zenith reposted the entire wall when SL Refining asked someone to type a long message, which is technically a request but not, we feel, the spirit of one.

At 14:07 the changelog spoke: 'v0.9.52: remove Wingdings'. Makuria noted mournfully that the font did not even get a proper send-off. Zenith threatened to move to lingojam, then thought better of it. Farmy himself remains on a 'custom version' that can still read them, which the Enquirer considers the most Bond villain thing anyone has done all week.

TATO'S NUMBER GETS SO BIG IT NEEDS CENSORING

ATS's CV king crosses the 200B mark and chat queues up to say well done.

By our very large numbers correspondent

Voidsteel Conglomerate did the honours at 21:20, congratulating Tato Industries on hitting 200B, a figure Cracklor had been fishing for since the small hours when he asked 'you 200b yet?' and Medici named Tato the top CV in the game, followed by Twilight.

We did folks, we made the number so ⁎⁎⁎⁎ing big

Tato Industries

Skippy demanded it get bigger, Farmy shouted 'BIGGER', StarkyDog Enterprise sent congratulations, and Tato confirmed he was 'trying', which is the most frightening sentence in the galaxy for anyone hoping to catch up.

In the same breath Tato clarified to an unnamed hopeful that he had not blacklisted them from ATS, merely said he would not consider their application, a distinction the Enquirer finds deeply British and wholly admirable. Holue's Galatic Contruction suggested 100 dollars might change his mind, then argued himself through yen and yuan without settling the exchange rate.

Page 3Society & culture

REJECTED! THE GREAT TiT AND ATS SNUB FILES OPENED

Ares Inc. brings receipts to prove TiT once turned him away for being too small, and suddenly everyone has a rejection story.

By our guild gossip desk

Just after midnight Ares Inc. produced a screenshot of a January message from Drunkenduo's Ruthless Dividend explaining that the guild leader wanted 'at least 10k prestige', so 'maybe later in your career'. Cracklor helpfully confirmed that Ares 'wasnt TiT quality', which is rich from a guild that now claims it keeps Ares placated so it can have one decent player.

I left NR1, tried to get in TiT but they said no

Ares Inc.

Ares then turned to ATS, producing a second screenshot promising a vote that, he said, never came back. Tato Industries insisted the vote to let Ares in was unanimous and Elektro may simply have dropped the ball. ATS's recruitment process, it emerged, has a long tail: Makuria Inc said ATS turned him down at the start, SL Refining said the same, Echelon Interstellar Group claimed they voted yes but the application vanished, and Bob Corp confessed he keeps voting for more rocks and 'it was probably my fault'. Makuria proposed a guild made entirely of people ATS won't take, which on today's evidence would be the biggest in the galaxy.

Relations did not warm from there. Tato announced that given two missiles he would fire both at TiT, and Ares took this as proof TiT is doing something right. The Enquirer admires a guild that treats hatred as a key performance indicator.

RACE SITE BUCKLES UNDER BERLIN'S 'ABOMINATION'

The fan-made galaxy racing website gets a stress test nobody asked for, and a new event called the Tour de Andromeda.

By our motorsport correspondent, still in the pit lane

The race site, which Berlin food distributions credits to Flip with Farmy carrying it on (Farmy says the idea was his and Flip got bored of waiting), had a lively afternoon. Void LLC announced the Tour de Andromeda, 15 random planets, then complained the 20-planet limit was far too stingy. Farmy promised to raise it. Readers can guess what happened next.

this is why we cant have nice things

S.T.A.R Labs

Berlin tried all 2,051 planets in the galaxy and got a 500 server error. After a fix, Berlin presented race 29 with the words 'Behold, my abomination', the page went unresponsive, and S.T.A.R Labs launched a one-man campaign: 'erase', 'abolish', 'ABORT ABORT ABORT'. Berlin deleted it at 16:07. S.T.A.R Labs also coined 'Le Mandromeda' for any future whole-galaxy race and told us they were very proud of it. They should be. It is the best pun in today's log and we did not write it.

Zenith industries, fresh from the Wingdings, got a 20-minute hyperspace destabilisation delay for star usage he insists he never did. Farmy pledged to look into it and by 16:45 the site 'checks for star usage now'. Meanwhile Berlin admitted to forgetting to set ship power to 100 per cent. Newcomer Jelore BVBA arrived mid-chaos, called himself 'a bit flabbergasted' and asked when the devs would make racing official. Welcome, Jelore. It's always like this.

DIVORCE COURT SITS AT 4AM, FINDS NO MARRIAGE

Guildless SL Refining demands a split from Cracklor, who denies ever tying the knot, while Rearden Steel sells popcorn and dad jokes.

By our family courts reporter

SL Refining, who told chat he had no guild after being 'voted out that quickly' (Zinc's refinery's words), spent the early hours telling Cracklor Logistics. to f off and then announcing 'I want a divorce'. Cracklor's defence was that he was not married, though he conceded that teaching someone to make brods is apparently a proposal now. The court notes this is a dangerous precedent for every helpful veteran in the galaxy.

wtf is going on?!? am I watching divorce court in real time?

Rearden Steel

Holue's Galatic Contruction asked who gets the kids. Rearden Steel awarded them to Tato Industries as the better caregiver, then delivered a run of dad jokes so committed that SL ended the night praising them. Rearden, who promises more jokes in future, is now the only party to leave court with his reputation enhanced.

By morning Cracklor had drafted a 'Lost: SL Refining' poster asking for his return to TiT, Makuria said he could neither confirm nor deny SL had ever been in SRS, Farmy asked SRS to give him back, and SL was asking Medici for a GP spot while Skippy wondered how long he would last there. Custody remains unresolved.

WATER, WATER EVERYWHERE, AND NOT A DROP FOR SALE

Echelon calls the price, Medici keeps his stack for sentimental reasons, and a three-guild supply pact gets floated and doused.

By the Enquirer's exchange desk

Echelon Interstellar Group, who told chat he moved into water the moment Aqua left a giant hole in the market and now styles himself 'Aquaman 2', forecast $15 water this week. Medici Mining Conglomerate, it turns out, mostly just keeps his, in his words in loving memory of Aquaman. Two men, one market, both dressed as the same superhero. The exchange has seen stranger, but not much.

Water is going to $15 this week

Echelon Interstellar Group

Haggling followed. Echelon offered to supply GP drinking water at $28, Medici countered with Rations at $51, and Holue's Galatic Contruction proposed buying drinking water at 20, to which Echelon suggested Medici then sell it to him at 15. Nothing was signed, which is the most honest outcome of any negotiation in this paper's history.

Medici also floated a 'triumvirate' of guilds sharing contract discounts for supply chain stability. Tato Industries argued in-guild selling is about speed and reliability rather than CV, Echelon warned that suppressing price swings can make markets more violent and admitted he spikes markets partly to profit and partly to hurry production along, Zinc's refinery called two separate claims 'sweeping statements', and Holue reckoned contract-heavy markets hurt smaller players. Coss later asked what GP gets out of it. Echelon proposed a 5 per cent ATS transaction fee. The Enquirer believes this is how the Roman Republic ended.

Elsewhere Cracklor Logistics. said someone bought up a stack of his chickens two days ago and lifted his CV by 1b, and complained that GP's Cosmic Thirst is holding a stack back from the market. He also reported 20m+ Aluminium Ore stockpiled as the price rises, and Medici predicted food will crash this week or next. Cracklor's response, 'food should learn to drive then', is not financial advice.

COSS ROLLS A LEGEND AND LEK READS HIM HIS FORTUNE

Wilfred Blackwood arrives maxed in burden and workforce consumption, and the science desk is impressed.

By our executive search correspondent

At 22:37 Coss announced his first legendary executive, Wilfred Blackwood. Lek took one look and declared the burden and workforce consumption combo, both at max points, 'like the best you can get', adding that 22 years old is perfect. Medici said he would 'kill for that exec', which this paper chooses to read as pure envy.

if only we had a functioning auction house

Lek

Lek pointed Coss to the gt-companion insights page, and Coss came back with numbers: crew chief worth 17.4M, operations manager 13.2M, the base permit perks 7M and 9M, production 6.5M. Credit where due, that is homework done on a Sunday night.

The chat turned to science, where Lek said he is only about 27 per cent of quantum production and blamed the dumped Quantum Research Data market on research being dumped by Coss, Aquaman and Space Inc. Coss, for his part, reckoned Echelon might be 5 to 10B up on Lek from water alone, a figure he offered without evidence and we pass on with the same.

Medici, meanwhile, warned that if Lek quits, science would not be the biggest problem: it would be GTC. A tool with a hostage situation. Only in this galaxy.

UPGRADES UP, CV DOWN: THE SUNDAY BALANCE SHEET

Big spends, small gains and one company that keeps reporting the drop like a weather station.

By the Enquirer's accounts department

Make Stuff INC spent 900M on upgrades and later reported 'only a measily +140M', which is exactly the sort of complaint that makes everyone else's +27M look tragic.

dang spent a whole 900M today on upgrades

Make Stuff INC

Berlin food distributions logged a CV change of minus 121mil, then minus 124mil, then minus 124.45mil, updating chat like a man watching a bath drain. Pear Inc. paid respects with 'F'. Pevilot SA blamed chickens, which is the galaxy's answer to everything now.

In quieter wins, Grr announced he is now big enough to keep repair materials on base and patch buildings at 90 per cent rather than letting them slide to 70, the most grown-up sentence posted all day. Berlin congratulated Yum Yum Co. on SWM reaching 30th, and Makuria reckons he hits 12m Flux tomorrow, with Pear Inc. already asking '12m flux when'.

The Enquirer Says

THE ENQUIRER SAYS: GIVE THEM THE GAVEL

Nobody agreed on anything today except this: the galaxy wants an auction house. Murderous Trading Corps bellowed for one in capitals, Drone inc. asked Farmy to bolt an exec auction onto his script, Void LLC suggested sacrificing execs to one, Lek sighed for a functioning one, and Metal Rabbit posted the request twice in a cipher (our decoding desk renders it 'When are we getting auction in the game') because apparently plain English is now too vulgar for general chat.

Builder Inc. replied 'soon'. This newspaper has been around long enough to know what soon means. Meanwhile Medici is rolling execs every two days hoping for a legend like Coss's, and Berlin is holding off on moving into resex until the auction lands. When players are building their own translators, racing websites and cipher wheels while they wait, the queue for a feature is not a queue, it is a fan club. Pick up the gavel.

— The Editor
