Pulse of the Galaxy

Chaotic, with a hint of desperation

The talk of Berlin food distribution's potential move to TiT has everyone guessing at the motives and outcomes of such a switch. Players are simultaneously wary and intrigued, as evidenced by the exchanges about prestige and the debate over wanqing files.

An undercurrent of desperation seeps into the chatter when players like Heisenberg lament the lack of interest in the arcane uses of wanqing and vinegar.

The day reads as a blend of chaotic potential moves and playful teasing of guilds and systems alike, leaving players in various states of excitement and anxious speculation.

Tit Attempts Guild Takeover

Berlin, S.T.A.R Labs gets dragged into the chaos.

By the Enquirer's gossip desk

Talk of Berlin joining TiT dominated chat all day. The move started when Heisenberg (TiTHeisenberg) challenged Berlin to join, and offered F4T food distribution (Berlin food distributions) the choice of ‘bring Berlin and scam’.

Heisenberg offered an hour of TiT experience if the Berlin player joined but was quickly shunned when TiT offered to let Berlin return.

When the matter turned to business, Heisenberg and Berlin debated the relative merits of prestige and arcade. Heisenberg noted that prestige is most valuable currency and dismissed the arcade as a money pit. Berlin player suggested he’d be bored ‘without Berlin anyway’.

Heisenberg, while being challenged by Berlin for their ‘tame’ behaviour, offered to kick Yum Yum (TiTYum Yum Chems) to make space for Berlin. But the space wasn’t there in the end, leaving Heisenberg to say, 'who berlin? GET IN HERE BERLIN.'

Heisenberg's Boosh Ban

Another day, another Heisenberg feud

By the Enquirer's crime desk

While debating the virtues of prestige and vinegar, Scam (F4T Scam) complained about being banned from booshing

Scam blamed Star, the system, calling them a 'ruthless dictator' and a 'saint.' The player added, ‘star banned me from booshing :(. He do be a ruthless dictator’.

Heisenberg, responding to the booshing debacle, said, ‘by the time we're done booshing they aren't 6 figures. True, they will be 10 figures, potentially 11,’ Heisenberg also dismissed Peach Inc. as an 'important future member to TiT,' indicating an internal power-struggle.

Wanqing Files Leaked

Files suggest TiT guilds aren't all what they're cracked up to be

By the Enquirer's culture desk

TiT’s most wanted wanqing files leaked out into the public domain, with members discussing ‘the real files’ versus ‘the fake’ ones posted online.

The files are a source of tension in TiT as members argued over how to protect the secrets while continuing to poke fun. TiTHeisenberg posted 'those aren't even the best ones,' while Cucklor Logistics declared that the files were 'fu****ing peak.'

Berlin food distributions suggested joining to look the files up, an offer which Heisenberg accepted but with the caveat that if they left, the player owed TiT one hour of prestige.
