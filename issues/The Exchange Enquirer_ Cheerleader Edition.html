Eleven minutes a dwarf

Applicant, cheerleader, criminal suspect, member, ex-member. Farmy McFarm Face The 7th finally got into RAS, then went home to TiT, which kicked him.

By the Enquirer's guild affairs desk

It began at 19:35 on Tuesday with a polite question to Rearden Steel: could he rock and stone? The groundwork had been laid minutes earlier, when Pear Inc. set out the entry terms for what he insists on calling the fruit basket. To get in, Farmy would have to rename himself jackfruit mc jacked pineapple the 8th. Farmy said ok. He then asked Errantes Inc. whether RAS had any space, helpfully pointing out that some of its members looked inactive, and adding that you can refuel for free if you have fuel.

At 19:42 Metal Rabbit confirmed the application was with the Fruit Basket Admissions Committee, and that chanting would not improve his position. Farmy asked whether they wanted him to chant. Bob Corp chanted anyway. Ten minutes later the committee rewarded Farmy's relentless enthusiasm with a promotion, from applicant to full-time RAS cheerleader, while the paperwork was reviewed.

Then Cracklor Logistics arrived. From 20:14 his evidence consisted largely of capital letters: why Farmy, why would you do it, you know what you did. Farmy said he did not. Cracklor took exception to both "yes?" and "no?" as answers. Metal Rabbit, sensing a development, invited Cracklor to share what Farmy had done, since it could influence the application.

"Your laughter has been duly noted as evidence of absolutely no remorse."Metal Rabbit, for the committee

At 20:33 the application was temporarily upgraded to a criminal investigation. Six minutes later Farmy was demoted from cheerleader and asked to return the costume. GreaVroes LLC offered the sharpest legal reading of the night: Farmy knows everything he did, he just doesn't know how much of it they found out. At 20:40 the charge was finally read out. Farmy, Cracklor revealed, had made an awesome game. Ares Inc. said he was very disappointed.

On Wednesday it happened. At 13:08 Farmy left TiT. At 13:09 AMY welcomed him to RAS. He reported that he had expected everyone to speak in dwarven voices, told KUR's Aranei, who had shouted "For Karl!", that he was in the wrong guild, and announced that he had found the RAS anthem.

By 13:20 he was back in TiT, where Cracklor asked who had let it back in. At 13:22 Ares Inc. kicked him. "My bad," said Ares. Farmy protested that Ares only kicks inactive players and people on holiday, and he was neither. He rejoined at 13:23. Cracklor, who saw the kick and decided it was someone else's problem, later explained the readmission: mum said he can't leave the dog outside.

The rations inquiry

Boosting allegations reach F4T, the accused pleads guilty with a cat face, and the defence says it's just the guild rate.

The week's second boosting scandal broke at 21:39 on Tuesday, when Mittcb of TiT alleged that orion constructs of F4T was being boosted by guildmate meowco, with rations priced 20% below the exchange average and worth up to $13M a day in profit. "Get the pitchforks out!" he wrote. Farmy demanded an explanation. meowco provided one: "I'm a booster :3."

The context matters. Minutes earlier meowco had claimed that everyone in TiT is boosted. SL Refining denied it, pointing out that he had asked to be boosted many times and got nothing. Mittcb said he had told the devs he identified as a new and small company in his starter bonus, and still wasn't boosted. The pitchforks, it turned out, came with a "/s" and an admission: Mittcb just wanted to be boosted too.

"What if boosting is just guild rate to the extreme? It's a slippery slope!"Mittcb, 22:04

orion constructs mounted a calm defence twenty minutes later. He buys four days of rations at a time because they restock every three or so days, and F4T sells rations to guildmates at 40. That might be boosting, he allowed, or it might just be the guild rate. meowco added that chemistry players don't need boosting anyway, because chem is good enough on its own.

Meanwhile, asked by Dumb whether TiT had a boosted player called Redshift, SL Refining corrected the record: TiT has more than one.

Page 3Business

Two minutes to fix iron. Construction still waiting.

At 3:43 in the morning Holue asked why iron was underproduced. By 3:45 it wasn't. Then he started on construction.

Holue of GR opened the small hours of Wednesday by asking why on earth iron was still underproduced. Zinc's refinery replied "im on it boss," and two minutes later reported that iron was now green. Holue checked metallurgy profits and concluded that the met players were making bank. Zinc called it the reward for sticking with metallurgy through the hard times, when iron sat below 200. meowco remembered it nearer 130 to 150 over the summer. Holue said not in the last few months.

Then came the real subject. Metallurgy, Holue said, at least has a real T1 industry that isn't mostly capped at stupid prices. Construction's problem, as he laid it out at 04:56: T3 demand is so volatile that any profit floods the market, T2 is often massively oversupplied, and T1 is mid but reliable with concrete and prefab. The moment structural elements or modern prefab kits get profitable, everyone switches their prefab lines and the price crashes.

"I will never stop complaining about t1 const fed caps."Holue, 04:32

The federal caps bite hardest, in his view, on amenities, construction kits and rations. He extended solidarity to food production over rations and drinking water, and to manufacturing over office supplies and construction vehicles, though he noted construction is hit three times harder than the rest combined. He has messaged the devs so many times he assumes they are sick of it.

Zinc took the other side. The capped goods are the ones new players need, so letting amenities cost 1.5 times as much would be bad for newcomers. A better fix would be cutting admin overhead for T2 to T4 workers, shifting demand away from T1 construction materials, rations and water. Amenities, he suggested, are best treated like uranium, a good not really meant for bulk production. He conceded that construction has always been the abandoned child of the specs, and that any fix he proposed would probably be ignored anyway.

SL Refining asked whether Holue ever stops complaining. Holue wants a gold construction medal before he switches, and may settle for bronze. Zinc's view of metallurgy as an alternative: stable, and requires zero brainpower.

Tesco, Lidl, or neither

A job interview is announced, disowned, and replaced by a job. Our correspondent had not slept.

At 09:23 on Wednesday, Murderous Trading Corps mentioned a job interview at 10:30. Farmy asked if it was at McDonald's. Absolutely not, came the reply: it was at Tesco, where there are no Uber drivers. This proved to be the least stable claim of the morning.

Farmy and Cracklor pointed out that Uber drivers are everywhere, including Tesco. Murderous said McDonald's Uber drivers are toxic, while KFC's are nice. Farmy, with rising volume, explained that they are all the same drivers, and that he had once seen a car with Just Eat, Uber Eats and Deliveroo bags in it at the same time. Cracklor had seen a driver with both. Murderous said he'd never seen one at Tesco, that he doesn't go to Tesco much, and that he prefers Aldi or Lidl for the bakery. Farmy observed that he was undermining his own argument.

"THEY ARE ALL THE SAME DRIVERS."Farmy, 09:26, no coffee

At 09:32 Murderous announced that he works at Lidl. Farmy asked if he was going insane and posted a screenshot of the Tesco message. Murderous said that wasn't him, that he had been on the toilet at the time, and that he needed to change his passwords. By 09:39 the official position was: no job interview, a job at Lidl, and Tesco is mid. He has not been to Tesco since 2019.

Cracklor wished him luck with the Tesco interview anyway, reassured him there is no shame in greeting, and advised practising the Farmy stare. Farmy, who had neither slept nor had his coffee, sold his rations and went back to work.

The Ultra Ball shortage

Medici's childhood sadness, he recalled, was failing to catch Zapdos in Pokémon Yellow because he hadn't brought enough Ultra Balls.
