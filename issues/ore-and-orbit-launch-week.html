Pulse of the Galaxy

Giddy, broke, and suspiciously familiar

A brand new galaxy, Ore & Orbit, opened its books this fortnight - built solo by a developer going by FlipCo, explicitly as a quality-of-life-improved answer to a certain other space economy game this paper has covered rather a lot. The first ten days of its general chat read almost exactly like Andromeda's own founding week: confused newcomers, a developer fielding every question personally, and an economy nobody quite understands yet.

The one genuinely new wrinkle is a pet chicken feature that has already produced a death, a funeral, and at least one accusation of infidelity.

AN AUSPICIOUS START

A NEW GALAXY OPENS, AND SOMEONE IMMEDIATELY ASKS WHAT THE LOG IS

Ore & Orbit's general chat opens exactly as these things always do

By the Gazette's founding desk

A new galaxy opened for business this fortnight: Ore & Orbit, built solo by a developer going by FlipCo, explicitly pitched to the chat as a quality-of-life-improved answer to a certain other space economy game this paper has spent the past year covering. General chat opened with a greeting, and then with a tycoon encountering the game's own debug output and reacting as any reasonable person would.

EW WHAT THE FUCK IS THAT LOGo

Starry Labrador

Nobody ever did explain what the log actually was. The galaxy simply moved on, as galaxies do, straight into guild recruitment drives, confused questions about production toggles, and a dev who somehow shipped new updates faster than the playerbase could read the old patch notes.

By day two there were three active guilds, a casino, and at least one tycoon who had already gambled away a four-figure sum he did not have.

THE GAMBLING ADDENDUM

A slot machine arrives "to balance the economy"; the economy remains unbalanced

Early in week one, FlipCo added slot machines to the exchange, reportedly to help balance the economy - a stated goal this paper treats with the scepticism it deserves. By his own account the feature had already cost the galaxy real money in testing alone.

no joke, in testing - I did a $10k spin on the slot

FlipCo

MeowCo asked, with commendable honesty, for a way to disable gambling entirely, citing an addiction and no money. Starry Labrador simply kept losing and announcing it to the whole server, between rounds of begging other players to buy his rations.

Ares Inc. offered what this paper considers the week's most accurate line of economic analysis, on the true nature of the game he had just joined.

THE GREAT COPPER BOTTLENECK

One miner, one smelter, and an entire ship-parts industry held hostage

Launch week's defining economic saga was copper. Farmy Mc Farm Face mined it practically alone for days while nobody else would touch it, repeatedly and increasingly desperately begging the rest of the galaxy to buy his stock, while Ares Inc. refused to buy on the grounds that his own copper ingots weren't selling either - a genuine supply-chain standoff that held up the entire ship-parts industry for the better part of a week.

prepare for the most expensive copper ingot the server will ever see

Ares Inc.

The deadlock finally broke once Gruender Inc. needed copper for engines and the whole chain - Farmy's ore, Ares's ingots, Cracklor's ship parts - clicked into gear at once. Ares, surveying the sudden demand, was unequivocal about what came next.

SELF-INFLICTED

ONE MAN'S RESEARCH-DATA EMPIRE

DrD spends launch week as the galaxy's only research salesman, and it costs him everything

TiT's DrD spent the week running what can only be described as a one-man research-data advertising agency, with slogans escalating by the hour - flash sales, limited offers, and at one point a straight-faced pitch promising to turn 'I dunno' into 'According to the data'.

My chicken left me

DrD

When a balance patch doubled research costs overnight, DrD's own verdict on the industry he had just spent a week championing was unusually candid, and considerably more personal than this paper was expecting.

THE SPEED PATCH NOBODY ASKED FOR

A balance change quietly tripled flight times galaxy-wide, and the galaxy noticed within the hour. FlipCo ran an actual chat poll asking what a reasonable rim flight should take; Ares Inc. voted for ninety minutes while simultaneously reporting his own fully loaded ships were taking four hours.

I enjoyed long flights. lol

FlipCo

FlipCo's own defence of the change did little to reassure a playerbase watching its starting speed bonus evaporate in real time.

THE RISE OF PEAR THE GREAT

A chat filter bug accidentally crowns a new deity

The week's single best accident happened when the chat filter's autocorrect quietly began converting any four-letter combination of P, E, A and R into 'PEAR THE GREAT', turning poor Pear Inc. into an unkillable in-chat phenomenon purely through typo physics. By the time anyone noticed, half the galaxy's attempts to spell ordinary words had been swallowed whole, and players were typing increasingly elaborate words just to watch them get eaten.

this is a PROFESH subreddit for the Plorlar Blrears aweomse gmae

Pear Inc.

Pear Inc. embraced the chaos completely, at one point founding an unofficial fan community he described in terms this paper is happy to reproduce in full.

THE CHOOKEM CHRONICLES

A pet chicken feature produces a death, a funeral and an accusation of infidelity inside a week

FlipCo's pet-chicken feature - officially a company mascot that mines ore and buffs ship speed - immediately became the week's emotional centre of gravity. Players fed, bred, lost and occasionally accidentally killed their own chickens, and a small industry of golden, alien and robot chicken-skin hunting sprang up almost overnight.

RIP Meteor. Long live Meteor the 2nd

Mittcb

The saga peaked with Meteor, Titan Forge Collective's chicken, dying of overenthusiastic affection, prompting a eulogy from Mittcb this paper considers genuinely moving for a tabloid about a pixel bird.

Separately, a minor chicken-fidelity scandal broke out when Mittcb's chicken Top Half Gary was discovered visiting Starry Labrador's chicken Rigel at the same time Rigel's own partner, Henrietta, was elsewhere. Pear Inc.'s verdict on the affair was blunt and entirely in capitals.

TWO RIVAL CALCULATOR SITES, ONE VERY TENSE TRUCE

Two fan-made companion sites launched within days of each other: Farmy Mc Farm Face's OO-Companion and Skippy's Orbit Companion. Both insisted they had built independently and neither would admit to copying the other, and the pair spent most of the week publicly roasting each other's load times and colour schemes.

It ended, as these things tend to, with a grudging truce and FlipCo cheerfully encouraging both to keep going.
